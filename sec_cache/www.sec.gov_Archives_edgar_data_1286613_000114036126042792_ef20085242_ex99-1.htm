Lincoln Educational Services Reports Strong Third Quarter Financial Results, Student Starts Increase More Than 14%, Adjusted EBITDA Grows Nearly 31%

Conference Call Today, at 10:30 a.m. Eastern Standard Time

PARSIPPANY, N.J., November 9, 2026– Lincoln Educational Services Corporation (Nasdaq: LINC) today reported financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026, as well as recent business developments.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Highlights
(Quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to quarter ended September 30, 2025, unless otherwise noted)

•
Student starts grew by 14.2% to 7,3621
•
Revenue increased 12.6% to $159.2 million from $141.4 million
•
Adjusted EBITDA increased 30.8% to $17.4 million from $13.3 million2
•
Net income increased 24.1% to $4.7 million, from $3.8 million
•
Diluted earnings per share increased to $0.15, from $0.12
•
Net cash from operating activities improved $11.0 million to $34.9 million versus $23.9 million last year
•
Total liquidity as of September 30, 2026, of approximately $136 million2
•
Ending student population rose by 11.4% to 20,325
•
Increased full-year 2026 revenue guidance

Year-to-Date 2026 Financial Highlights
(Nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to September 30, 2025, unless otherwise noted)

•
Revenue increased 18.7%, or $70.3 million, to $445.7 million
•
Adjusted EBITDA increased 49.0% to $45.6 million from $30.6 million2
•
Net cash provided by operating activities increased to $61.5 million from $15.8 million
•
Student starts increased 11.0% to 18,840
•
Average student population rose by 14.4% to 18,4041

Recent Business Developments

•
Completed the acquisition of previously leased Melrose Park, Illinois campus property for $18.8 million.
•
Signed new leases for an approximately 90,000 square-foot facility in Tempe, Arizona, which will be the Company’s first campus in the state and is expected to open in the first quarter of 2028, and for an approximately 36,000 square-foot focused-program facility in Suitland, Maryland, expected to open in the fourth quarter of 2027.
•
During the third quarter, the Company repurchased 871,135 shares of its common stock for approximately $21.0 million.
•
Our Board approved a $30 million increase in the existing share repurchase authorization, to $70 million in total, with more than $37 million remaining available.
•
In November, the Company entered into an amendment under its revolving credit facility that provides, among other items, greater flexibility for daily borrowing and repayment and enhanced share repurchase flexibility.
•
The new Hicksville, New York, campus, the second in the state, is expected to begin its first class in November.

“During the third quarter, we generated double-digit growth across all our key metrics, highlighted by our 14.2% increase in student starts” said Scott Shaw, President & CEO. “The strong starts reflect our organization’s flexibility and responsiveness to a dynamic marketing environment where we continue to see robust demand for our high return-on-investment training and education programs. We continue to examine effective strategies to broaden our future lead generation sources and we saw an improvement in lead conversion rate to starts during the quarter to our historic levels. Based on our year-to-date performance and current outlook, we are refining our guidance for start growth of 10% to 12% for the full year, within the previous guidance range we established following our first quarter.

“Our start-growth and increase in average student population led to solid third quarter revenue growth of 12.6%. During the period, we continued to realize operating efficiencies in many of our operations, which contributed to a 22.5% increase in operating income, 30.8% growth in Adjusted EBITDA and a 24.1% rise in net income.

“The strong cash generation, and enhanced liquidity continues to fund our growth initiatives. Our new Hicksville, NY campus is expected to begin classes this month while the construction of our Rowlett, Texas campus continues. During the quarter, we signed leases and advanced plans to develop campuses in the metropolitan Washington, D.C. and Phoenix, Arizona, markets. Our business development efforts to examine other underserved markets for both our full scale and focused-program campus models remain quite active. We also continue with our efforts to enter new partnerships or expand existing ones as the demand for skilled workers remains far higher than supply. For instance, our WorkforceLinc Division recently expanded our collaboration with CMC Corporation and entered a new partnership with ABB to train students for careers in electrical systems and technology careers serving the data center industry.

“As we look to the remainder of 2026 and the longer-term future, based on our operational performance through October and current trends, we are raising our full-year revenue guidance. The need for employees trained in the automotive, skilled trades, and healthcare fields continues its significant growth, and Lincoln Tech is providing an established, proven education and training model to help America address its chronic skills gap. Our momentum increases our confidence in achieving our long-term 2030 objectives of $850 million in revenue and $150 million of EBITDA as we continue to expand our leadership position,” Mr. Shaw concluded.

2026 THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS

(Quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to quarter ended September 30, 2025)


 •
Revenue increased by $17.8 million, or 12.6%, to $159.2 million, primarily due to a 10.9% increase in average student population.


•
Educational services and facilities expense increased by $9.8 million, or 17.2%, to $67.1 million. This includes a $3.1 million increase in costs related to the new campuses in Houston, Hicksville, Rowlett, and Suitland. The increase was primarily driven by costs associated with a larger student population, increased laptop pricing, and higher depreciation expense, largely resulting from capital investments to support growth initiatives.


•
Selling, general and administrative expense increased by $6.5 million, or 8.4%, to $84.3 million driven by higher student population and costs related to new campuses.

Corporate and Other

This category includes unallocated expenses incurred on behalf of the entire Company. Corporate and other expenses were $17.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $17.0 million in the prior year period and remain relatively consistent year over year.

2026 YEAR-TO-DATE FINANCIAL RESULTS

(Nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to nine months ended September 30, 2025)


 •
Revenue increased by $70.3 million, or 18.7%, to $445.7 million, primarily due to a 14.4% increase in average student population.


•
Educational services and facilities expense increased by $33.7 million, or 22.2%, to $185.1 million, primarily driven by a larger student population, costs related to new campuses, and higher depreciation expense, largely resulting from capital investments to support growth initiatives.


•
Selling, general and administrative expense increased by $31.4 million, or 14.8%, to $243.1 million, primarily driven by a larger student population, higher sales and marketing expense, and costs related to new campuses and other growth initiatives.

Corporate and Other

Corporate and other expenses were $56.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $51.7 million in the prior year period. The increase was primarily driven by higher salaries and benefits to support a larger student population and to execute the Company’s growth initiatives.

ABOUT LINCOLN EDUCATIONAL SERVICES CORPORATION

Lincoln Educational Services Corporation is a leading provider of diversified career-oriented post-secondary education helping to provide solutions to America’s skills gap. Lincoln offers career-oriented programs to recent high school graduates and working adults in four principal areas of study: skilled trades, automotive, health sciences and information technology. Lincoln has provided the workforce with skilled technicians since its inception in 1946 and currently operates 23 campuses in 12 states under the brands Lincoln Technical Institute, Lincoln College of Technology and Nashville Auto Diesel College. The Company was incorporated in New Jersey in 2003 as the successor-in-interest to various acquired schools including Lincoln Technical Institute, Inc. which opened its first campus in Newark, New Jersey in 1946. For more information, please go to www.lincolntech.edu.

(1) RECONCILIATION OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

In addition to disclosing financial results that are determined in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company believes it is useful to present non-GAAP financial measures that exclude certain significant items as a means to understand the performance of its business, and to enable comparability of operating performance between periods. Additionally, the Company’s management regularly uses these non-GAAP financial measures to make operating decisions, and for planning and forecasting purposes. EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, and total liquidity are measures not recognized in financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP.


 •
We define EBITDA as income (loss) before net interest expense (interest income), provision (benefit) for income taxes, depreciation and amortization.

•
We define adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA plus stock-based compensation expense and adjustments for items not considered part of the Company’s normal recurring operations.

•
We define total liquidity as the Company’s cash and cash equivalents and available borrowings under our revolving credit facility.

EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, and total liquidity are presented because we believe they are useful indicators of the Company’s performance and ability to make strategic investments and meet capital expenditures and debt service requirements. However, they are not intended to represent cash flows from operations as defined by GAAP and should not be used as an alternative to net income (loss) as indicators of operating performance or cash flow as a measure of liquidity. EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, and total liquidity are not necessarily comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.

Adjusted EBITDA excludes non-cash stock-based compensation and one-time, non-recurring items. Historically Adjusted EBITDA has excluded pre-opening costs, as well as net operating losses from new campuses, for up to four quarters after the campus opening, or until the campus becomes profitable, whichever occurs first. Beginning in fiscal year 2026, the Company no longer excludes pre-opening costs or net operating losses from new campuses and program expansions when calculating adjusted EBITDA. Going forward, adjusted EBITDA will reflect only the add-back of non-cash stock-based compensation and other non-recurring items, if any. Prior period amounts in this release have been recast to conform to the current methodology.
