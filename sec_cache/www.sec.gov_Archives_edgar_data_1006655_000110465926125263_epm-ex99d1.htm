Management Comments

Kelly Loyd, President and Chief Executive Officer, commented: "We are encouraged by our fiscal first quarter results and excited about the outlook for our diversified portfolio, particularly the increasing contributions from our growing mineral and royalty interests. Our recently acquired Permian Minerals exceeded production expectations, and our late fiscal 2026 acquisitions of Haynesville and Bossier Shale minerals and royalties are contributing in line with expectations as new wells enter production. We bought these assets for both their existing production and their substantial development potential and are encouraged to see that potential begin to translate into results at the end of the fiscal quarter. Looking ahead, with a full quarter of Permian

Minerals ownership and additional wells coming online there as well as in the Haynesville and Bossier Shales, we expect our recent investments to meaningfully add to cash flow in the coming quarters.

“We continue to unlock value from acreage that contributes little to no current production. After quarter-end, we sold 636 net acres from our roughly 40,000-net-acre Williston position for $2.5 million while retaining a 0.25% overriding royalty interest in up to 6-8 miles of horizontal completions. Together with last quarter’s $3.0 million sale of nonproducing mineral acreage, these transactions demonstrate our ability to convert potential future value into cash today with minimal impact on current production or cash flow. We are also negotiating additional potential farmouts of nonproducing working interest acreage across multiple areas of our portfolio and hope to announce further agreements soon.”

Our near-term focus is on realizing the benefits of our recent investments, strengthening our balance sheet, and sustaining our quarterly dividend. We see opportunities for continued growth within our existing portfolio, which sets a high bar for new investments as we focus on translating those opportunities into greater value per share.”

Fiscal First Quarter 2027 Financial Results

Oil, natural gas, and natural gas liquids revenues increased 6% to $22.6 million, up from $21.3 million in the year-ago quarter. The change was driven primarily by an 11% increase in average realized equivalent prices, partially offset by a 5% decrease in average daily production.

Lease operating costs ("LOE") decreased to $12.4 million compared to $13.1 million in the year-ago quarter. The decrease reflected lower operating costs at TexMex following a change of operator since the prior-year quarter, as well as lower taxes and gathering and transportation costs associated with decreased natural gas prices at Jonah Field and Barnett Shale. On a per-unit basis, LOE was $19.25 per BOE compared to $19.45 per BOE in the year-ago quarter, partly reflecting the addition of minerals and royalty (“M&R”) assets, which have lower operating costs per BOE.

Depletion, depreciation, and accretion expense was $5.7 million compared to $6.0 million in the year-ago period. On a per-BOE basis, the Company's current quarter depletion rate was $8.32 per BOE, compared to $8.26 per BOE in the year-ago period.

General and administrative ("G&A") expenses (excluding stock-based compensation) increased to $2.1 million from $1.8 million in the year-ago quarter due to higher professional fees and an increase in employee salaries and benefits. On a per-BOE basis, G&A (excluding stock-based compensation) was $3.22 compared to $2.66 in the year-ago period, in part due to lower production volumes.

The Company reported net losses of $3.0 million, or $0.08 per share, compared to net income of $0.8 million, or $0.02 per share, in the year-ago period. Excluding the impact of selected items, which include a net loss on the unrealized portion of hedges, the Company reported an adjusted net loss of $0.3 million, compared to an adjusted net loss of $0.1 million in the year-ago period.(1)

Total production for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 decreased to 6,978 net BOEPD compared to 7,315 net BOEPD in the year-ago period. Total production for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 included approximately 1,848 barrels per day ("BOPD") of crude oil, 3,956 BOEPD of natural gas, and 1,174 BOEPD of NGLs. Production decreased in the current quarter primarily due to unplanned downtime at Delhi and TexMex, as well as natural decline across the portfolio, partially offset by M&R acquisitions in the Haynesville and Bossier Shales, and the Permian, and development on the mineral acreage in the SCOOP/STACK. In addition, the prior-year period includes flush production from wells brought online at Chaveroo Field, which was 100% crude oil.

The Company's average realized commodity price (excluding the impact of derivative contracts) increased 11% to $35.13 per BOE in fiscal Q1, compared to $31.63 per BOE in the year-ago period.

Operations Update

This quarter, the Company integrated its Permian Minerals assets into its portfolio, and since the August 20th acquisition date, the Company has production data confirmed for 21 gross wells. As of quarter-end, the Company has 853 gross PDP wells, 45 gross wells in progress, 50 gross permitted wells, and over 1,200 additional drilling locations across its Permian position. Evolution expects an increased contribution of production and cash flow, as additional wells are brought online, and the Company will be able to report a full quarter of ownership.

At SCOOP/STACK, new well activity continued across Evolution's working interest and M&R positions, resulting in flat production sequentially. LOE decreased in total and on a per-unit basis compared to the year-ago quarter, in part due to the addition of Evolution’s M&R interests in August of 2025, which incur lower operating costs than the Company’s working interest assets. During fiscal Q1, two gross wells were brought online, eight gross wells were in progress, and the Company had elected to participate in three additional gross working-interest wells.

The Company’s aggregate production from the Jonah, Barnett, Williston, and Hamilton Dome properties increased slightly over the prior quarter, demonstrating the value of high-quality, long-life reserves.

In Louisiana, production from the Company's Haynesville and Bossier Shale M&R interests continued to come online. During the quarter, Evolution acquired approximately $0.3 million of additional Haynesville and Bossier Shale M&R interests, primarily targeting undeveloped acreage with near-term third-party operator activity. As of quarter-end, the Company had 92 gross PDP wells, an additional 42 gross PUD locations, and an estimated 50 additional drilling locations across its Haynesville and Bossier Shale M&R portfolio. The Company continues to benefit from this activity without funding drilling and completion costs.

Balance Sheet, Liquidity, and Capital Spending

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $3.1 million, outstanding borrowings of $60.0 million, and $0.8 million in letters of credit outstanding under its Senior Secured Credit Facility, with a weighted average interest rate of 6.48%. As of quarter-end, availability under the facility was $12.2 million, bringing total liquidity to $15.3 million. In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, Evolution paid $4.9 million in common stock dividends, $16.0 million for the Permian Minerals Acquisitions, and incurred $0.7 million in capital expenditures. These cash outlays were partially offset by $12.5 million in net proceeds from the Company's August 2026 public equity offering, $3.5 million in net borrowings under its Senior Secured Credit Facility, and $3.3 million in net cash provided by operating activities. These figures do not include approximately $2.5 million in proceeds received from the first Williston Basin farmout transaction that closed in November 2026.

On August 20, 2026, Evolution entered into a letter agreement with MidFirst Bank pursuant to which the borrowing base on the Company's Senior Secured Credit Facility was temporarily increased from $65.0 million to $73.0 million to provide additional capacity for the Permian Minerals acquisition. On October 20, 2026, under the terms of the Senior Secured Credit Facility, it was redetermined at $70.0 million based on the semi-annual review of all the Company's properties. As of October 20, 2026, availability under the facility was $9.2 million.

Cash Dividend on Common Stock

On November 9, 2026, Evolution's Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.12 per share of common stock, payable on December 31, 2026, to common stockholders of record on December 15, 2026. This will be the 53rd consecutive quarterly cash dividend on the Company's common stock since December 31, 2013. To date, Evolution has returned approximately $156.6 million, or $4.65 per share, back to stockholders in common stock dividends.

Non-GAAP Reconciliation – Adjusted EBITDA (Unaudited)

(In thousands)

Adjusted EBITDA and Net income (loss) and earnings per share excluding selected items are non-GAAP financial measures that are used as supplemental financial measures by our management and by external users of our financial statements, such as investors, commercial banks, and others, to assess our operating performance as compared to that of other companies in our industry, without regard to financing methods, capital structure, or historical costs basis. We use these measures to assess our ability to incur and service debt and fund capital expenditures. Our Adjusted EBITDA and Net income (loss) and earnings per share, excluding selected items, should not be considered alternatives to net income (loss), operating income (loss), cash flows provided by (used in) operating activities, or any other measure of financial performance or liquidity presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Our Adjusted EBITDA and Net income (loss) and earnings per share excluding selected items may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of another company because all companies may not calculate Adjusted EBITDA and Net income (loss) and earnings per share excluding selected items in the same manner.

​

We define Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) plus interest expense, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation, depletion, and accretion (DD&A), stock-based compensation, ceiling test impairment, and other impairments, unrealized loss (gain) on change in fair value of derivatives, and other non-recurring or non-cash expense (income) items.
