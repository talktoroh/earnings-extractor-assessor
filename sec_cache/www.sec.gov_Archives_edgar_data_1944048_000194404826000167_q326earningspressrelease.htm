Kenvue Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

•Delivered Fourth Consecutive Quarter of Net and Organic Sales1 Growth

•Net Sales Increased 3.2%; Organic Sales Increased 2.9%

•Diluted EPS Increased 5% to $0.22; Adjusted Diluted EPS1 Increased 4% to $0.29


SUMMIT, N.J. October 30, 2026 – Kenvue Inc. (NYSE: KVUE) today announced financial results for the fiscal third quarter ended September 27, 2026.

“We are pleased to have delivered our fourth consecutive quarter of net and organic sales growth, with strong performance in Self Care and Essential Health, along with margin expansion and strong cash generation,” said Kirk Perry, Chief Executive Officer. “Our results reflect continued momentum from disciplined execution, ongoing productivity initiatives, and investment in our brands and innovation. We are strongly on track to deliver our business transformation that we began last year. I want to thank our entire organization for their dedication to delighting our consumers and customers, disciplined execution, and delivering what was promised.”

Perry continued, “As we near the completion of our value-creating combination with Kimberly-Clark and bring together our two companies, the team is excited about the opportunities ahead.”

Third Quarter Summary
•Net sales increased 3.2% vs the prior year period, reflecting Organic sales growth of 2.9% and a foreign currency benefit of 0.3%.
•Gross profit margin was 59.5% vs 59.1% in the prior year period. Adjusted gross profit margin1 was 61.5% vs 61.2% in the prior year period.
•Operating income margin was 18.2% vs 16.7% in the prior year period. Adjusted operating income margin1 was 21.9% vs 21.5% in the prior year period.
•Diluted earnings per share were $0.22 vs $0.21 in the prior year period. Adjusted diluted earnings per share1 were $0.29 vs $0.28 in the prior year period.
•Due to the pending transaction with Kimberly-Clark, the Company will not be providing forward-looking guidance.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Net Sales and Organic Sales
Third quarter 2026 Net sales increased 3.2% vs the prior year period, primarily reflecting Organic sales growth of 2.9% and a foreign currency benefit of 0.3%. Organic sales growth was driven by volume growth of 1.6% and favorable value realization of 1.3%.

Gross Profit Margin and Operating Income Margin
Third quarter 2026 Gross profit margin was 59.5% vs 59.1% in the prior year period. Adjusted gross profit margin was 61.5% vs 61.2% in the prior year period. The year-over-year change in both measures primarily reflects savings from productivity gains attributable to our global supply chain optimization initiatives, U.S. tariff refunds, and favorable value realization, partially offset by the impact from inflation as well as unfavorable transactional foreign exchange.

Third quarter 2026 Operating income margin was 18.2% vs 16.7% in the prior year period. Third quarter 2026 Adjusted operating income margin was 21.9% vs 21.5% in the prior year period. The year-over-year change in both measures reflects the year-over-year change in Gross profit margin and Adjusted gross profit margin, as well as savings from our cost optimization actions, including the 2026 Restructuring Initiative and Our Vue Forward, partially offset by a year-over-year increase in brand support.

Interest Expense, Net and Taxes
Third quarter 2026 Interest expense, net was $89 million vs $93 million in the prior year period.

Third quarter Effective tax rate was 29.4% vs 24.3% in the prior year period. The Adjusted effective tax rate1 was 24.7% in the current period vs 24.0% in the prior year period. The year-over-year increase in both measures largely reflects a reduction in favorable discrete return-to-provision adjustments as compared to the prior year, partially offset by the impact of tax law changes.

Net Income Per Share (“Earnings Per Share”)
Third quarter 2026 Diluted earnings per share were $0.22 vs $0.21 in the prior year period. Adjusted diluted earnings per share were $0.29 in the current period vs $0.28 in the prior year period.

Third Quarter 2026 Business Segment Results

Self Care
Third quarter 2026 Net sales increased 5.9% vs the prior year period, reflecting Organic sales growth of 5.4% and a foreign currency benefit of 0.5%. Organic sales growth was driven by a volume increase of 3.4% and favorable value realization of 2.0%. Organic sales increased in Asia Pacific (“APAC”), benefiting from favorable inventory-related comparisons following prior year trade inventory depletion, while strong innovation and commercial execution in Pain Care enabled Motrin® to outperform the market as the region experienced rising flu incidences. Smoking Cessation delivered low double-digit Organic sales growth in the quarter, led by Europe, Middle East, and Africa (“EMEA”) and APAC, reflecting strong demand and commercial execution for Nicorette®. Pepcid® continued to outperform the category, benefitting from strong commercial and innovation-led initiatives. In the U.S., consumption trends improved sequentially, and Tylenol® gained market share despite lower seasonal incidences versus the prior year period and continued category softness.

Skin Health and Beauty
Third quarter 2026 Net sales increased 0.1% vs the prior year period, reflecting Organic sales decline of 0.5% and a foreign currency benefit of 0.6%. Organic sales decline was driven by a volume decrease of 1.7%, partially offset by favorable value realization of 1.2%. Organic sales declined vs the prior year period reflecting distribution losses and timing of promotions in the U.S. and the impact of retailer negotiations in Europe. Hair Care was a key growth driver during the quarter, delivering low double-digit Organic sales growth, led by Rogaine®, which achieved its fourth consecutive quarter of market share gains, and continued momentum behind OGX®. Innovation continued to contribute to Organic sales performance across all regions, including the expansion of North America Sun Care with the launch of Neutrogena® Cloud TechTM featuring BEMT (bemotrizinol) and the brand's proprietary technology, continued expansion of Neutrogena® Ultra Sheer® Sun, and momentum behind OGX® ProGrowth and ProGrowth + Peptide platforms.

Essential Health
Third quarter 2026 Net sales increased 2.5% vs the prior year period, reflecting Organic sales growth of 2.5%. Organic sales growth was driven by a volume increase of 2.0% and favorable value realization of 0.5% with growth across all regions. Baby Care delivered growth for the quarter driven by Johnson’s Baby® across all regions supported by social commerce acceleration, strong activations, and innovation. Women’s Health also contributed to growth, led by Stayfree®, which benefited from recent product launches in India, and o.b.®, which returned to growth in EMEA following its brand restage and pricing actions. Wound Care continued to gain market share, supported by BAND-AID® Brand PRO HEAL® 5 Day Protect, distribution expansion, and retailer
3

activations. Innovation remained a key growth driver, including Stayfree® Secure Nights and XpertXL in India, the ongoing rollout of Listerine® On-The-Go mouthwash packets, and Johnson’s Baby® innovations in Latin America.

Cash Flow and Balance Sheet
Fiscal nine months ended September 27, 2026 Net cash flows from operating activities were $1.9 billion vs $1.3 billion in the prior year period, with the improvement driven by an increase in net income and by lower use of working capital. Capital expenditures were $0.3 billion vs $0.4 billion in the prior year period, as Free cash flow1 increased to $1.6 billion vs $1.0 billion in the prior year period. Total cash and cash equivalents were $1.0 billion as of September 27, 2026 vs $1.1 billion as of December 28, 2025. Total debt was $8.2 billion as of September 27, 2026 vs $8.5 billion as of December 28, 2025.

2026 Restructuring Initiative
As previously disclosed, on February 17, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors approved an initiative (the “2026 Restructuring Initiative”) that aims to optimize its operating model, transform its supply chain, reduce complexity, and drive operational efficiencies, while strengthening core capabilities. The Initiative is expected to result in pre-tax restructuring expenses and other charges totaling approximately $250 million in fiscal year 2026.

No Conference Call
Due to the pending transaction with Kimberly-Clark, Kenvue will not be hosting a quarterly conference call. This press release will be posted on the Company’s website at investors.kenvue.com.

About Kenvue
Kenvue Inc. is the world’s largest pure-play consumer health company by revenue. Built on more than a century of heritage, our iconic brands, including Aveeno®, BAND-AID® Brand, Johnson’s®, Listerine®, Neutrogena®, and Tylenol®, are science-backed and recommended by healthcare professionals around the world. At Kenvue, we realize the extraordinary power of everyday care. Our teams work every day to put that power in consumers’ hands and earn a place in their hearts and homes. Learn more at kenvue.com.

Adjusted effective tax rate: We define Adjusted effective tax rate as U.S. GAAP Effective tax rate adjusted for the tax effects on special item adjustments including amortization of intangible assets, restructuring expenses and operating model optimization initiatives, Separation-related costs, conversion of stock-based awards, Founder Shares, Pending Transaction and other related costs, and costs associated with the Skillman sale-leaseback. Adjusted net income excludes the impact of items that may obscure trends in our underlying performance. Management believes this non-GAAP measure is useful to investors as the Company uses Adjusted net income for strategic decision making, forecasting future results, and evaluating current performance.

Adjusted operating income: We define Adjusted operating income as U.S. GAAP Operating income adjusted for amortization of intangible assets, restructuring expenses and operating model optimization initiatives, Separation-related costs, conversion of stock-based awards, Founder Shares, Pending Transaction and other related costs, costs associated with the Skillman sale-leaseback, and the impact of the Deferred Markets. Management believes this non-GAAP measure is useful to investors as management uses Adjusted operating income to assess the Company’s financial performance.

Market Share Information
The Company uses market share and related metrics, including Average Weekly Recommendations, as indicators to assess business performance and trends. Market share references in this press release are derived from a combination of consumption and market share data provided by third-party vendors and internal estimates. Unless otherwise indicated, such references represent the percentage of the dollar value of sales of our products, relative to all product sales in the category in the countries in which the Company operates and purchases data.

Market share data is subject to inherent limitations, including the availability and timing of underlying information. In particular, market share data is not generally available for certain retail channels. The Company measures market share through the most recent period for which market share data is available, which generally reflects a lag time of one or two months. While the Company believes the third-party vendors it uses to provide data are reliable, it has not independently verified the accuracy or completeness of such data or its underlying assumptions. In addition, the Company’s reported market share data may differ from that reported by other companies due to differences in category definitions, geographic scope, internal estimates and other factors.
