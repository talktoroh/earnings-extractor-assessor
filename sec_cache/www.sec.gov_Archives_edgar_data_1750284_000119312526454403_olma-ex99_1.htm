Olema Oncology Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results

 

•
On track to announce top-line data from the pivotal Phase 3 OPERA-01 trial in the first quarter of 2027
•
Continued strong enrollment in the pivotal Phase 3 OPERA-02 trial and the Phase 1b/2 study of OP-3136
•
Appointed Jason O’Byrne, MBA, as Chief Financial Officer
•
Ended the third quarter with $413.8 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities

 

SAN FRANCISCO, November 10, 2026 (Globe NewsWire) – Olema Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“Olema” or “Olema Oncology”, Nasdaq: OLMA), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on the discovery, development, and commercialization of targeted therapies for breast cancer and beyond, today reported financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“As we approach top-line data from our pivotal Phase 3 OPERA-01 trial in the first quarter of 2027, we remain confident in palazestrant’s potential to emerge as a differentiated endocrine therapy in second- and third-line metastatic breast cancer,” said Sean P. Bohen, M.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Olema Oncology. “Turning to our pivotal Phase 3 OPERA-02 trial in first-line metastatic breast cancer, momentum continues to build. Enrollment is accelerating, investigator interest is strong, and patients with frontline disease continue to require better therapeutic options. We believe that palazestrant, in combination with ribociclib, is well positioned to become the new standard of care in this setting. With its dual mechanism of action as a complete estrogen receptor antagonist and selective estrogen receptor degrader, optimal pharmacokinetic properties, encouraging clinical profile demonstrated to date, and potential for use both as a monotherapy and in combination with other agents, we believe palazestrant is uniquely positioned to transform the metastatic breast cancer treatment paradigm.”

 

Bohen continued, “Beyond preparing for the potential approval and commercialization of palazestrant, we remain encouraged by the potential of OP-3136, our novel KAT6 inhibitor, as it advances through clinical development across multiple indications and treatment combinations. We were also pleased to welcome Jason O’Byrne as Chief Financial Officer this quarter. His extensive experience in corporate strategy, capital markets, and financial management and operations will be invaluable as we prepare for key catalysts in 2027 and continue our transformation into a fully integrated oncology company.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, were $413.8 million.

 

Net loss for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $73.6 million, as compared to $42.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in net loss for the third quarter was primarily related to increased patient enrollment in our OPERA-01 Phase 3 clinical trial, strong patient enrollment in our OPERA-02 Phase 3 clinical trial, continued advancement of our Phase 1b/2 OP-3136 clinical program, research activities, increased manufacturing and clinical supply activities in support of advancing clinical programs and early preparations for the potential commercialization of palazestrant and higher personnel related costs.

 

GAAP research and development (R&D) expenses were $67.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $40.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in R&D expenses was primarily related to increased spending on the continued enrollment of our pivotal Phase 3 OPERA-01 and OPERA-02 clinical trials for palazestrant, increased manufacturing and clinical supply activities supporting these programs, continued advancement of our Phase 1 OP-3136 clinical program, and higher personnel-related costs to support expanding development activities, including an increase in non-cash stock-based compensation expense of $5.1 million, mainly due to higher grant prices in 2026 and higher headcount.

 

Non-GAAP R&D expenses were $59.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, excluding $7.7 million non-cash stock-based compensation expense. Non-GAAP R&D expenses were $37.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, excluding $2.6 million non-cash stock-based compensation expense. A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures used in this press release can be found at the end of this press release.

 

GAAP G&A expenses were $9.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $5.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The increase in G&A expenses primarily reflects continued investment in personnel and corporate infrastructure to support our expanding late-stage clinical development activities, and early preparations for the potential commercialization of palazestrant, including an increase in non-cash stock-based compensation expense of $2.7 million, mainly due to higher grant prices in 2026, and an increase in professional fees of $1.0 million.

 

Non-GAAP G&A expenses were $5.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, excluding $4.4 million non-cash stock-based compensation expense. Non-GAAP G&A expenses were $4.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, excluding $1.7 million non-cash stock-based compensation expense. A reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures used in this press release can be found at the end of this press release.

 

About Olema Oncology
Olema Oncology is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company committed to transforming the standard of care and improving outcomes for patients living with breast cancer and beyond. Olema is advancing a pipeline of novel therapies by leveraging our deep understanding of endocrine-driven cancers, nuclear receptors, and mechanisms of acquired resistance. Our lead product candidate, palazestrant (OP-1250),

 

is a proprietary, orally available complete estrogen receptor antagonist (CERAN) and a selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD), currently in two Phase 3 clinical trials. In addition, Olema is developing OP-3136, a potent lysine acetyltransferase 6 (KAT6) inhibitor, now in a Phase 1 clinical study. Olema is headquartered in San Francisco and has operations in Cambridge, Massachusetts. For more information, please visit www.olema.com.

 

About Palazestrant (OP-1250)
Palazestrant (OP-1250) is a novel, orally available small molecule with dual activity as both a complete estrogen receptor antagonist (CERAN) and selective estrogen receptor degrader (SERD). It is currently being investigated in patients with recurrent, locally advanced or metastatic ER-positive (ER+), human epidermal growth factor receptor 2-negative (HER2-) breast cancer. In clinical studies, palazestrant completely blocks ER-driven transcriptional activity in both wild-type and mutant forms of metastatic ER+ breast cancer and has demonstrated anti-tumor efficacy along with attractive pharmacokinetics and exposure, favorable tolerability, central nervous system penetration, and combinability with cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6 (CDK4/6) inhibitors. Palazestrant has been granted U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Fast Track designation for the treatment of ER+/HER2- metastatic breast cancer that has progressed following one or more lines of endocrine therapy with at least one line given in combination with a CDK4/6 inhibitor. It is being evaluated as a single agent in the ongoing pivotal Phase 3 clinical trial, OPERA-01, and in combination with ribociclib in the ongoing pivotal Phase 3 clinical trial, OPERA-02. Palazestrant is also being evaluated in multiple Phase 1/2 studies in combination with ribociclib, palbociclib, alpelisib, everolimus, and atirmociclib.

 

About OP-3136
OP-3136 is a novel, orally available small molecule that potently and selectively inhibits lysine acetyltransferase 6 (KAT6), an epigenetic target that is dysregulated in breast and other cancers. In preclinical studies, OP-3136 has demonstrated significant anti-proliferative activity in ER+ breast cancer models and is combinable and synergistic with endocrine therapies including palazestrant and cyclin-dependent kinase 4/6 (CDK4/6) inhibitors. The Investigational New Drug (IND) application for OP-3136 was cleared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in December 2024 and patients are currently enrolling in the Phase 1 clinical study.
