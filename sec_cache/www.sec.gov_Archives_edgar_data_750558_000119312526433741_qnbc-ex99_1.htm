QNB CORP. REPORTS

THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

 

QUAKERTOWN, PA (October 27, 2026) QNB Corp. (the “Company” or “QNB”) (NASDAQ: QNBC), the parent company of QNB Bank (the “Bank”), reported a net loss for the third quarter of 2026 of $14,774,000 or a loss of $2.67 per share on a diluted basis. QNB announced the launch and pricing of an underwritten public offering on August 19, 2026. The offering was completed on August 20, 2026 for the issuance of a total of 1,232,142 common shares which added $48,284,000, proceeds net of underwriting discounts and related costs, to shareholders' equity. QNB announced on September 8, 2026 the execution of a strategic repositioning of a portion of its securities available-for-sale portfolio (the "balance sheet restructuring"). QNB sold $250,712,000 in available for sale securities at a loss of $29,049,000 and unwound the related swap for a gain of $1,336,000. QNB also previously announced the acquisition of Victory Bancorp, Inc. ("Victory"), a highly complementary community banking franchise headquartered in Limerick, Pennsylvania, was officially closed on April 1, 2026. Results for the three and nine months of 2026 include three and six months, respectively, of post-merger activity related to the acquisition of Victory. The merger-related costs are significant one-time costs and are not normal recurring operating expenses.

 

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 included a net loss of $27,713,000 due to the balance sheet restructuring and merger-related costs of $251,000 compared to $519,000 in the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the impact of the balance sheet restructuring and the merger-related costs, net of tax of $21,981,000, adjusted net income was $7,207,000 and adjusted diluted earnings per share was $1.30*. This compares to adjusted net income, excluding the merger-related costs, net of tax, of $410,000 of $4,058,000, or $1.09* per share on a diluted basis, for the same period in 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, QNB reported a net loss of $8,994,000, or a loss of $1.89 per share on a diluted basis. Net income included merger-related costs, net of tax, of $3,249,000. Excluding the impact of the balance sheet restructuring and the merger-related costs, adjusted net income was $16,236,000 and adjusted diluted earnings per share was $3.39*. This compares to net income, excluding merger-related costs, of $10,519,000, or $2.83* per share on a diluted basis, reported for the same period in 2025.

 

For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, the annualized rate of return on average assets (ROAA) and average shareholders’ equity (ROAE) was negative 2.44% and a negative 29.70%, respectively. Adjusted ROAA and ROAE, excluding the impacts of the balance sheet restructuring and merger-related costs, for the three-month period of 2026 was 1.19%* and 14.49%*, respectively. This compares with adjusted ROAA and ROAE, excluding the impact of merger-related costs 0.85%* and 13.89%*, respectively, for the third quarter 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, QNB reported ROAA and ROAE of a negative 0.54% and a negative 7.03%, respectively. Adjusted annualized ROAA and ROAE, excluding the impacts of the balance sheet restructuring and merger-related cost, for the nine-month period of 2026 was 0.97%* and 12.68%*, respectively. This compares with adjusted ROAA and ROAE, excluding the impact of merger-related costs 0.74%* and 12.68%*, respectively, for the same period in 2025.

 

The operating performance of the Bank, a wholly-owned subsidiary of QNB Corp., included three months of post-merger activity and the impact of the balance sheet restructuring during the third quarter of 2026. Excluding the impacts of the balance sheet restructuring and merger related costs, net of tax, at the Bank, the operating performance at the Bank improved for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, in comparison with the same period in 2025, due primarily to improvement in the interest margin causing a $6,532,000 increase in net interest income and a $927,000 increase in non-interest income; this was partly offset by an increase in non-interest expense of $2,709,000. The contribution from QNB Corp., which included three months of post-merger activity, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, declined compared with the same period in 2025, primarily due to a decrease in net interest income of $369,000, related to the subordinated debt acquired in the acquisition, a decrease in merger-related costs of $355,000 and an increase in other non-interest expense of $123,000.

Total assets as of September 30, 2026 were $2,361,620,000 compared with $1,906,005,000 at December 31, 2025. Loans receivable increased to $1,772,174,000 and total deposits increased to $2,056,299,000.

 

“Our third-quarter results reflect a period of significant strategic progress for QNB. We have strengthened our capital position through our public offering," said Dave Freeman, President and Chief Executive Officer. "While reported earnings were impacted by the balance sheet restructuring and final merger-related expenses, the underlying performance of our franchise remained strong. Adjusted earnings improved meaningfully compared to the prior year, supported by higher net interest income, margin expansion, strong loan growth, and the successful integration of Victory Bancorp, where we are realizing meaningful synergies, particularly through robust loan growth. Importantly, the balance sheet restructuring positions us to benefit from further earnings improvement in future periods. These actions have strengthened our balance sheet, enhanced our market presence, and positioned QNB for continued growth and profitability.”

 

Net Interest Income and Net Interest Margin

Net interest income for the quarter ended September 2026 totaled $19,161,000, an increase of $6,163,000, from the same period in 2025. Tax-equivalent net interest margin was 3.25% for the third quarter of 2026 and 2.72% for the same period in 2025, an increase of 53 basis points. Tax-equivalent net interest margin was 3.09% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with 2.64% for the same period in 2025.

 

The yield on earning assets was 5.31% for the third quarter of 2026 compared to 4.91% for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 40 basis points. For the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026, the yield on earning assets was 5.15%, compared with 4.87% for the same period in 2025; an increase of 28 basis points.

 

The cost of interest-bearing liabilities was 2.52% for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with 2.64% for the same period in 2025, a decrease of 12 basis points. For the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026, the cost of interest-bearing liabilities was 2.50%, compared with 2.70% for the same period in 2025, a decrease of 20 basis points.

 

Quarterly average loan growth of $509,380,000 was offset by an increase in average deposits of $397,272,000, an increase in subordinated debt of $14,708,000 and an increase in shareholders' equity of $81,475,000, primarily due to the acquisition of $408,379,000 in loans, $409,165,000 in deposits and $47,101,000 in equity were added upon the close of the Victory merger. Additionally, equity was impacted by the $48,284,000 in net proceeds from the public offering.

 

Loan growth was primarily in commercial real estate, which comprised 56.0% of average earning assets in the third quarter of 2026 compared with 46.3% for the same period in 2025, and the increases in both rates and volume in commercial real estate loans contributed to the 24 basis-point increase in the yield on loans. The average rate paid on interest-bearing deposits decreased 13 basis points. The 41-basis point decrease in the rate on short-term borrowings was due to both rate and volume.

 

Asset Quality, Provision for Credit Losses on Loans and Allowance for Credit Losses

 

QNB recorded a $379,000 provision for credit losses on loans in the third quarter of 2026 compared to a $98,000 provision in the third quarter of 2025. QNB recorded a $900,000 provision for credit losses on loans in the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to a $504,000 provision in the same period of 2025. QNB added $3,020,000 in allowance for credit losses due to the acquisition in the second quarter of 2026. QNB's allowance for credit losses on loans of $13,047,000 represents 0.74% of loans receivable at September 30, 2026, compared to $9,215,000, or 0.73% of loans receivable at December 31, 2025. Net loan charge-offs were $102,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with $12,000 for the same period in 2025. Net charge-offs for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $88,000 compared with recoveries of $7,000 for the same period of 2025.

 

Total non-performing loans, which represent loans on non-accrual status and loans past due 90 days or more and still accruing interest, were $11,688,000, or 0.66% of loans receivable at September 30, 2026, compared with $8,793,000, or 0.70% of loans receivable at December 31, 2025. The increase was primarily due to four commercial and one retail customer. In cases where there is a collateral shortfall on non-accrual loans, specific reserves have been established based on updated collateral values even if the borrower continues to pay in accordance with the terms of the agreement. At September 30, 2026, $8,282,000, or approximately 71% of the loans classified as non-accrual, are current or past due less than 30 days. Commercial loans classified as substandard or doubtful loans totaled $44,621,000 at September 30, 2026, compared with $39,219,000 at December 31, 2025, an increase of $5,402,000 primarily due to commercial real estate and commercial and industrial loans acquired.

 

Non-Interest Income

 

Total non-interest income for 2026 includes three months for the quarter and six months for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 of impact from the acquisition. Noninterest income was a negative $24,951,000 for the third quarter of 2026 compared with $1,847,000 for the same period in 2025; and a negative $21,011,000 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared with $5,083,000 for the same period of 2025. Non-interest income for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 includes the negative impact of the balance sheet restructuring of $27,713,000. Non-interest income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 also included $96,000 of realized gains on the sales of investment securities and a $303,000 loss on the termination of an interest-rate swap acquired in the acquisition, both are unrelated to the balance sheet restructuring. The Bank completed

the exchange offer to convert its Visa B-2 shares to B-3 and C shares in the second quarter of 2026; the Bank subsequently converted the remaining two-thirds of the Visa C shares to Visa A shares and recorded a $612,000 unrealized gain; a total of $880,000 for the nine months of 2026. Excluding these gains and losses, non-interest income increased $303,000 to $2,150,000 from 1,847,000 for the third quarter; and increased $946,000 to $6,029,000 from $5,083,000 for the nine-month period.

 

Fees for services to customers increased $169,000 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as overdraft fees increased $53,000 and other deposit-related fees increased $116,000. ATM and debit card income increased $111,000. Retail brokerage and advisory income decreased $61,000 for the same period. Gains on sales of loans decreased $39,000 due to volume of sales. Other non-interest income increased $123,000 for the same period due to an increase in bank-owned life insurance of $48,000, an increase in letter of credit fees of $42,000, an increase other loan servicing fees of $20,000 and an increase in credit card income of $7,000.

 

Fees for services to customers increased $408,000 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as overdraft fees increased $150,000 and other deposit-related fees increased $258,000. ATM and debit card income increased $283,000. Retail brokerage and advisory income increased $9,000 for the same nine-month period. Gains on sales of loans decreased $17,000 due to volume of sales. Other non-interest income increased $263,000 for the nine-month period due to an increase in bank-owned life insurance of $105,000, an increase in letter of credit fees of $85,000, an increase in other loan servicing fees of $29,000, an increase in title company income of $26,000 and an increase in credit card income of $20,000.

 

Non-Interest Expense

Total non-interest expense for 2026 includes three and six months of impact from the acquisition for the third quarter and nine-month period ending September 30, 2026, respectively. Total non-interest expense was $12,734,000 for the third quarter of 2026 compared with $10,182,000 for the same period in 2025. Excluding pre-tax merger-related costs, non-interest expense increased $2,820,000 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to the same period in 2025. Total non-interest expense was $40,308,000 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared with $29,113,000 for the same period in 2025. Excluding pre-tax merger-related costs, non-interest expense increased $7,491,000 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the same period in 2025.

 

Salaries and benefits expense increased $1,640,000 to $6,888,000 in the third quarter of 2026, compared to the same period in 2025. Salary expense and related payroll taxes increased $1,401,000 to $6,017,000 and benefits expense increased $239,000 to $871,000 when comparing the two periods.

 

For the third quarter of 2026, net occupancy and furniture and equipment expense increased $304,000 to $1,992,000 as rental expense increased $156,000, depreciation increased $74,000, maintenance, utilities and costs increased $74,000. Other non-interest expense for the third quarter increased $876,000 due to increases in third-party services of $79,000, core deposit amortization of $332,000, marketing expense of $159,000, FDIC insurance of $119,000, courier expense increased $53,000, bank shares tax of $41,000, business development cost increased $35,000, communications and supplies increased $27,000, and various other expenses increased a net total of $34,000. Nine-month results for non-interest expense are similar to those discussed for the third quarter of 2026.

 

Income Taxes

Provision for income taxes decreased $5,048,000 to a benefit of $4,126,000 in the third quarter of 2026 and decreased $5,153,000 to a benefit of $2,602,000 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, due to lower taxable income, compared with the same periods in 2025. The effective tax rate increased for both the three- and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026 to 21.8% and 22.4%, respectively, from 20.2% and 20.2% for the same periods in 2025, respectively, due non-taxable merger-related costs.

 

About the Company

 

QNB Corp. is the holding company for QNB Bank, which is headquartered in Quakertown, Pennsylvania. QNB Bank currently operates fourteen branches in Bucks, Lehigh and Montgomery Counties along with two loan production offices in Montgomery and Berks Counties. The Bank offers commercial, small business, and personal customers banking services, borrowing solutions, and cash management tools in the communities they serve. In addition, the Company provides securities and advisory services under the name of QNB Financial Services through a registered Broker/Dealer and Registered Investment Advisor, and title insurance as a member of Laurel Abstract Company LLC. More information about QNB Corp. and QNB Bank is available at QNBBank.com.
