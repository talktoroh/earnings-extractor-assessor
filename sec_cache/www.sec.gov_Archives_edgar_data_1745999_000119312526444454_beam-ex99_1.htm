Beam Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights Clinical Progress Across Base Editing Portfolio

 

Updated BEAM-302 Phase 1/2 Clinical Data Presented at ERS Demonstrated Durable Increases in Functional AAT and Reductions in Mutant Z-AAT; Completion of Dosing in the Global Pivotal Cohort Expected in First Half of 2027

 

U.S. Biologics License Application (BLA) Submission for Risto-cel in Sickle Cell Disease Expected as Early as Year-End 2026

 

Dosing in Phase 1/2 Trial of BEAM-304 in PKU Anticipated to Initiate in Early 2027

 

Initial BEAM-301 Phase 1/2 Data Presented at ESGCT Demonstrated Clinical Proof of Concept for Genetic Correction of R83C Mutation in GSDIa; Enrollment in 60 mg Cohort Underway

 

Ended Third Quarter 2026 with $1.0 Billion in Cash, Cash Equivalents and Marketable Securities; Cash Runway Expected to Support Operating Plans into Mid-2029

 

Cambridge, Mass., November 3, 2026 – Beam Therapeutics Inc. (Nasdaq: BEAM), a biotechnology company developing precision genetic medicines through base editing, today reported third quarter 2026 financial results and provided updates across the company’s hematology and genetic disease franchises.

“Our progress this quarter reflects the growing maturity and momentum of Beam’s platform, with predictable clinical translation across four distinct programs and multiple potentially transformative medicines advancing toward patients, bringing our vision for a new era of genetic medicine increasingly within reach,” said John Evans, chief executive officer of Beam Therapeutics. “We are approaching a potential first BLA filing with risto-cel as early as the end of this year, and the pivotal cohort for BEAM-302 is rapidly advancing with completion of dosing expected in the first half of 2027. Recently, initial BEAM-301 clinical trial data showed exciting early evidence of clinical activity and provided further clinical validation of our in vivo LNP platform. Together with the planned initiation of BEAM-304 dosing in early 2027, these milestones reinforce our confidence in a platform designed to translate predictably, carry knowledge forward from one program to the next, and progressively expand the number of patients who can benefit from base editing.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Progress and Anticipated Milestones

 

Liver-targeted Genetic Diseases

 

BEAM-302: BEAM-302, Beam’s lead genetic disease program, is an investigational base editing therapy designed to be a differentiated, first-in-class liver-targeting therapy for alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency (AATD) that directly corrects the root cause of the disease and has the potential to address both liver and lung manifestations.

•
In September, Beam presented updated clinical data from the Phase 1/2 trial of BEAM-302 at the European Respiratory Society (ERS) Congress 2026, reinforcing its potential as a one-time treatment for both the lung and liver manifestations of AATD. With up to 18 months of follow-up, the data demonstrated durable increases in total and functional AAT, decreased circulating neutrophil elastase activity, and reductions in mutant Z-AAT and toxic circulating Z-polymers following a single dose. Evidence of AAT production under normal physiological control further supports BEAM-302’s potential to restore the body’s natural protective response, with a safety profile consistent with lipid nanoparticle (LNP)-based therapies.
•
Enrollment and dosing continue in the global pivotal cohort of the ongoing Phase 1/2 trial, which is designed to support a potential accelerated approval path in the United States. The company expects to complete dosing in the pivotal cohort in the first half of 2027.

 

 

 

BEAM-304: BEAM-304 is an investigational base editing program designed to leverage Beam’s proprietary base editing technology and LNP delivery capabilities to directly and durably correct mutations in the phenylalanine hydroxylase (PAH) gene that cause phenylketonuria (PKU). Beam aims to advance BEAM-304 using a platform approach to develop mutation-specific base editors for the majority of patients with PKU.

•
Beam nominated its third base editor within the BEAM-304 program targeting a third undisclosed PKU mutation. Now encompassing three different base editors for distinct mutations, the company believes the BEAM-304 program has the potential to address up to 50% of PKU patients in the U.S., with ongoing research effort to address additional pathogenic mutations.
•
Beam continues clinical start-up activities for the planned Phase 1/2 trial of BEAM-304. The trial will initially evaluate safety, tolerability and reduction of blood phenylalanine (Phe) levels in patients with the R408W mutation, with the goal of establishing clinical proof of concept for base editing in PKU. The trial is designed to subsequently evaluate base editors for additional mutations within a single clinical program. Beam expects to initiate dosing in the Phase 1/2 trial in early 2027.

 

BEAM-301: BEAM-301, an investigational base editing therapy, aims to correct one of the most common disease-causing mutations, R83C, in patients with glycogen storage disease type Ia (GSDIa).

•
In October, Beam presented initial clinical data from the ongoing Phase 1/2 trial of BEAM-301 at the European Society of Gene & Cell Therapy (ESGCT) Congress. Initial BEAM-301 clinical data demonstrated dose-dependent improvements in time to fasting hypoglycemia (TTH) across all patients, with two of three patients in the 40 mg cohort achieving the protocol-defined maximum controlled fasting duration of ≥15 hours, consistent with restored endogenous glucose production, and complete elimination of starch supplementation. BEAM-301 demonstrated an acceptable safety profile across both doses, consistent with the known risks associated with LNP-based therapies.
•
Based on the safety and clinical findings observed to date, Beam is enrolling patients in a 60 mg cohort of the ongoing open-label Phase 1/2 dose-exploration study and plans to engage with the U.S. FDA regarding a potential path to approval.

 

Hematology

 

Risto-cel: Ristoglogene autogetemcel (risto-cel, formerly known as BEAM-101) is an investigational autologous cell therapy with a potentially differentiated profile for the treatment of sickle cell disease (SCD).

•
Beam expects to report updated clinical data by year-end 2026 and submit a U.S. biologics license application (BLA) for risto-cel as early as year-end 2026.

 

Next-generation Programs in Sickle Cell Disease and Hematology:

•
Beam continues to make significant investments in developing targeted LNPs to deliver gene editing to hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs). Targeted LNPs for HSC delivery have been identified and are in advanced lead optimization.

Corporate Updates

•
In September, Beam appointed Eric Foster as chief commercial officer to lead the company’s commercial organization and strategy as its hematology and genetic disease programs advance toward potential launches. Mr. Foster is a seasoned biopharmaceutical executive with more than 25 years of commercial leadership experience, including senior roles at Ardelyx, Horizon Therapeutics, and GSK.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities were $1.0 billion as of September 30, 2026, compared to $1.2 billion as of December 31, 2025.
•
Research & Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $110.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $109.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•
General & Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $31.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $26.7 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•
Net Income (Loss): Net loss was $134.4 million, or $1.28 per share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net loss of $112.6 million, or $1.10 per share, for the third quarter of 2025.

Cash Runway

Beam expects that its cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, together with an additional $200 million expected to be drawn from the company’s facility with Sixth Street, will fund anticipated operating expenses and capital expenditure requirements into mid-2029, assuming satisfaction of the conditions applicable to those additional borrowings. This runway is expected to support the company through the potential launch of risto-cel in SCD, execution of the BEAM-302 pivotal development plan in AATD, and clinical proof of concept for BEAM-304 in PKU.

 

 

About Beam Therapeutics

Beam Therapeutics (Nasdaq: BEAM) is a biotechnology company committed to establishing the leading, fully integrated platform for precision genetic medicines. To achieve this vision, Beam has assembled a platform with integrated gene editing, delivery and internal manufacturing capabilities. Beam’s suite of gene editing technologies is anchored by base editing, a proprietary technology that is designed to enable precise, predictable and efficient single base changes, at targeted genomic sequences, without making double-stranded breaks in the DNA. This has the potential to enable a wide range of therapeutic editing strategies that Beam is using to advance a diversified portfolio of base editing programs. Beam is a values-driven organization committed to its people, cutting-edge science, and a vision of providing lifelong cures to patients suffering from serious diseases.
