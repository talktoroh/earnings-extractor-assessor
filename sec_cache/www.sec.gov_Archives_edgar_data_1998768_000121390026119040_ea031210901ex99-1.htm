PROFICIENT AUTO LOGISTICS REPORTS

Third quarter 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

 

JACKSONVILLE, FLORIDA – November 12, 2026 — Proficient Auto Logistics, Inc. (NASDAQ: PAL) (the “Company” or “Proficient”) today reported its financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Summary

 

Total Operating Revenue of $149.0 million, increased 30.3% from Q3 2025

 

Total Operating Income (Loss) of ($3.9) million, versus ($0.1) million in Q3 2025

 

Adjusted Operating Income(1) of $0.3 million, versus $4.2 million in Q3 2025

 

Adjusted Operating Ratio(1) of 99.8% compared to 96.3% in Q3 2025

 

Total Units delivered of 839,036, an increase of 38.6% from Q3 2025

 

Rick O’Dell, Proficient’s Chief Executive Officer, commented, “While our third-quarter results reflected healthy underlying demand, performance today does not yet reflect the earnings potential of this business, and we are focused on closing that gap and delivering for shareholders. We are encouraged by the opportunities ahead and are taking decisive actions to strengthen profitability and cash generation. The combination with Hansen & Adkins is creating a stronger, more scalable platform, and the significant increase in the synergy opportunity we have identified reinforces our confidence in the potential of the combined company. As we execute on the integration, improve pricing and fuel recovery and drive greater efficiency across our network, we believe we have a clear path to improved financial performance and long-term value creation.”

 

Hansen & Adkins Integration and Synergies Update

 

The Hansen & Adkins integration is progressing on schedule, with the Company on track to substantially complete integration by the end of the first quarter of 2027. Proficient is executing 11 integration workstreams focused on leveraging the combined network, fleet and purchasing scale. To date, significant actions have been completed including increasing sister hauling to reduce empty miles and improve utilization, opening 20 maintenance locations across the combined fleet, implementing common fleet and maintenance systems and consolidating fuel, insurance and other purchasing programs. These efforts are already generating incremental revenue, operating efficiencies and cost savings.

 

Through this work, Proficient has identified a total synergy opportunity of $20 million to $30 million, significantly above the initial $6 million to $10 million identified at announcement. The Company expects to achieve the high end of its initial synergy range by year-end 2026, with additional opportunities from resource and portfolio optimization, real estate, insurance and SG&A consolidation expected in 2027 and further optimization and automation benefits through 2029.

Third quarter revenue increased $34.7 million, or 30.3%, compared to the same quarter of 2025, while total unit deliveries were up 38.6% versus the same period of 2025, primarily reflecting the post-acquisition contribution of Hansen & Adkins beginning August 13, 2026.

 

The (10.3%) lower revenue per unit year-over-year for the third quarter (excluding fuel surcharge and other revenue) reflects portfolio mix and a proportionally shorter length of haul in the Hansen & Adkins portfolio as compared to the rest of the Proficient portfolio. Revenue per unit including fuel surcharge and other revenue was (6.0%) lower year-over-year as higher fuel surcharge recoveries partially offset the mix impact. Company unit deliveries increased to 46% of revenue in the quarter, reflecting the addition of Hansen & Adkins company drivers, continued prioritization of Company-owned truck asset utilization, and reduced available Subhauler capacity from supply that has exited the market.

 

Adjusted Operating Ratio was 99.8% in the third quarter compared to 96.3% in Q3 2025, reflecting elevated cost inflation and fuel surcharge under-recovery that more than offset pricing and productivity initiatives. Cost pressures remained significant across insurance, driver wages, recruiting and retention, and equipment maintenance. In addition, OEM-administered fuel surcharge programs did not fully recover increased fuel expense due to structural limitations and timing lag. Fuel surcharge under-recovery negatively impacted third-quarter operating ratio by an estimated 200 to 250 basis points.

 

Balance Sheet

 

The Company ended the third quarter with $11.8 million of cash and $140.6 million of senior secured debt (inclusive of $9.9 million drawn against its line of credit), resulting in net debt of approximately $128.8 million as of September 30, 2026. The increased indebtedness reflects the significant addition of Hansen and Adkins in mid-August and the related assumption of their equipment financing. In addition, convertible notes with a seven-year term were added at the time of the acquisition will be more fully described in our form 10-Q.

 

Share Repurchase Program

 

On March 2, 2026, the Company announced that its Board of Directors authorized a share repurchase program under which the Company may repurchase up to $15 million of its common stock. The repurchase program authorizes the Company to purchase its common stock from time to time in the open market, in block transactions, in privately negotiated transactions, through accelerated stock repurchase programs, through option or other forward transactions or otherwise, all in compliance with applicable laws, rules, regulations and other restrictions. As of the end of the third quarter, we have repurchased 181,678 shares of common stock at an average price of $5.89 for a total of $1.1 million.

About Proficient Auto Logistics

 

Headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, Proficient Auto Logistics (NASDAQ: PAL) is the leading specialized freight company focused on providing auto transportation and logistics services. Through the combination of nine industry-leading operating companies, including four since IPO debut May 2024, PAL operates the largest auto transportation fleet in North America, offering a broad range of services primarily focused on transporting finished vehicles from automotive production facilities, marine ports of entry, and regional rail yards to auto dealerships around North America. Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

We report our financial results in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). However, management believes that certain non-GAAP measures, including EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Operating Income, and Adjusted Operating Ratio, provide useful information in measuring operating performance, generating future operating plans and making strategic decisions regarding allocation of capital. Management believes this information presents helpful comparisons of financial performance between periods by excluding the effect of certain non-cash and non-recurring items.

 

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Operating Income, and Adjusted Operating Ratio do not have a standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP and therefore it may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies, and it should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP.

 

EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) for the period adjusted for interest expense, income tax expense (benefit) and depreciation expense and intangible amortization expense.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) for the period adjusted for interest expense, net, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization expense, stock compensation expense and any non-recurring items that management does not consider indicative of ongoing operating performance, including restructuring charges of $1.2 million recorded during the third quarter of 2025, non-cash goodwill impairment of $27.8 million recorded during the fourth quarter of 2025 and acquisition costs of $6.5 million recorded in 2026.

 

Adjusted EBITDA Margin is calculated as Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of operating revenue.

 

Operating income is calculated as total operating revenue less total operating expenses.

 

Adjusted operating income is calculated as total operating revenue less total operating expenses adjusted to exclude amortization of intangibles, stock compensation expense, and non-recurring items that management does not consider indicative of ongoing operating performance, including non-cash goodwill impairment of $27.8 million recorded during the fourth quarter of 2025.

 

Operating ratio is calculated as total operating expenses as a percentage of operating revenue.

 

Adjusted operating ratio is calculated as total operating expenses adjusted to exclude amortization of intangibles, stock compensation expense, and any non-recurring items that management does not consider indicative of ongoing operating performance, as a percentage of operating revenue.
