FTAI Aviation Ltd. Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 Performance and Announces $500.0 Million Accelerated Share Repurchase

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Net income attributable to shareholders up 26.8% from Q2 2026 to $149.1 million
•Adjusted EBITDA up 14.8% from Q2 2026 to $348.3 million
•Announced a $500 million accelerated share repurchase, doubled authorization to $1.0 billion and repurchased $80 million of shares since authorization in September
•Reaffirmed 2026 and 2027 Adjusted EBITDA guidance across all business lines
•Declared a $0.50 quarterly dividend per ordinary share
NEW YORK, October 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – FTAI Aviation Ltd. (Nasdaq: FTAI) (the “Company” or “FTAI”) today reported financial results for the third quarter 2026. The Company’s consolidated comparative financial statements and key performance measures are attached as an exhibit to this press release.
“FTAI delivered another strong quarter. Aerospace Products delivered outstanding growth with margins in line with our guidance, SCI’s new vehicle is now investing, and the Mod-1 has cleared its critical testing milestones ahead of first deliveries next year,” said Joe Adams, Chairman and CEO. “We have momentum across every part of the business and clear visibility into 2027, which is why our Board doubled our share repurchase authorization to $1.0 billion and why we announced an accelerated share repurchase alongside our regular quarterly dividend.”
Aerospace Products
•Generated Aerospace Products revenue of $910.0 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $282.4 million in Q3 2026, increases of 76% and 57%, respectively, compared to Q3 2025(1)
•Delivered an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 31%, in line with the Company's 30% margin guidance(1)(2)
•Produced 311 CFM56 modules across the global MRE network in Q3 2026, up 50% compared to Q3 2025
•Year-to-date production now totals 877 modules toward the 2026 outlook of 1,200 modules(2)
Aviation Leasing (and Strategic Capital)
•Strategic Capital now represents more than 50% of Aviation Leasing, reflecting the success of the Company's planned transition to an asset-light model(2)
•SCI I (“2025 SPV”) has closed 212 of 301 committed aircraft through September 30, 2026 and is expected to close substantially all committed aircraft by year end; paid a 5.0% distribution in Q3 2026, bringing year to date distributions to 17.5% of commitments(2)
•SCI II ("2026 SPV") started investing in September 2026 and ended the quarter with 109 aircraft committed; closed on its $2.0 billion committed warehouse facility in August(2)
Power
•More than 100 units under contract for delivery in 2027 and 2028 to customers in the U.S. and Canada(2)
•Completed the Mod-1's critical testing milestones, meeting its targets of 25MW for peak power and 23MW for continuous baseload power on both natural gas and liquid fuel(2)
•Entered into a joint venture to develop a mobile combined cycle add-on that increases Mod-1 output by 9MW and raises efficiency to above 50% with no additional fuel, emissions or water; first test units expected in the second half of 2027(2)

Third Quarter 2026 Dividends
The Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) declared a cash dividend on its ordinary shares of $0.50 per share for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, payable on November 23, 2026 to the holders of record on November 10, 2026.
Additionally, the Board declared cash dividends on its Fixed-Rate Reset Series D Cumulative Perpetual Redeemable Preferred Shares (“Series D Preferred Shares”) of $0.59375 per share for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, payable on December 15, 2026 to the holders of record on December 1, 2026.
Share Repurchase Program and Accelerated Share Repurchase
On October 27, 2026, the Board approved a $500.0 million increase to the Company’s share repurchase program, bringing the total authorization to $1.0 billion. Under the program, which was initially authorized on September 15, 2026, the Company has repurchased approximately $80.0 million of its ordinary shares to date at a weighted average price of $178.73 per share, including $50.0 million during the third quarter of 2026.
In connection with the increased authorization, the Company intends to enter into an accelerated share repurchase agreement (the “ASR”) with Royal Bank of Canada to repurchase $500.0 million of its ordinary shares. The Company expects to complete the transaction during the fourth quarter of 2026. Following completion of the ASR, approximately $420.0 million will remain available under the share repurchase program which will expire in 2027.

Key Performance Measures
In addition to net income (loss), the Chief Operating Decision Maker (“CODM”), who is the Company’s Chief Executive Officer, utilizes Adjusted EBITDA as a key performance measure. Adjusted EBITDA is not a financial measure in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“U.S. GAAP”). This performance measure provides the CODM with the information necessary to assess operational performance and make resource and allocation decisions. We believe Adjusted EBITDA is a useful metric for investors and analysts for similar purposes of assessing our operational performance.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) attributable to shareholders, adjusted (a) to exclude the impact of provision for (benefit from) income taxes, equity-based compensation expense, acquisition and transaction expenses, losses on the modification or extinguishment of debt and preferred shares and capital lease obligations, asset impairment charges, incentive allocations, depreciation and amortization expense, interest expense and dividends on preferred shares, internalization fee to affiliate, FTAI Power pre-commercial research and development (b) to include the impact of our pro-rata share of Adjusted EBITDA from unconsolidated entities and (c) to exclude the impact of equity in earnings (losses) of unconsolidated entities, if any.
Reconciliations of forward-looking non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are not included in this press release because the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures are not available on a forward-looking basis without unreasonable effort.
