EXHIBIT 99.1 

 

Zomedica Announces Record Third Quarter Revenue of $9.9 Million, Up 22% Year-Over-Year, Net Loss Reduced 48%, with $41 Million in Liquidity

 

Zomedica surpasses $9 million in quarterly revenue for the second quarter in a row while extending its record of consecutive year-over-year revenue growth to 23 quarters.

 

ANN ARBOR, MI / ACCESSWIRE / November 4, 2026 / Zomedica Corp. (OTCQB: ZOMDF) ("Zomedica" or the "Company"), an animal health company offering innovative point-of-care diagnostic and therapeutic products for equine and companion animals, today reported consolidated financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Zomedica delivered record third quarter revenue, continued year-over-year growth across our business, and made further progress in managing and improving upon operating costs and cash consumption.” said Larry Heaton, Chief Executive Officer of Zomedica.

 

“Our results reflect sustained demand for our PulseVet® and Assisi® therapeutic device products, increasing adoption of our diagnostic offerings, and contributions from our Development Services segment. Together, these businesses continue to strengthen our commercial foundation and expand our opportunities for long-term growth.

 

“Our core commercial business, excluding Development Services, grew 18% during the quarter and 14% year to date. Diagnostic revenue increased 113% compared to the third quarter of 2025, supported by continued commercial initiatives, our collaboration with Boehringer Ingelheim Animal Health, and expansion into additional international markets. We also entered into an agreement this quarter with Veterinary Study Groups (VMG), creating an opportunity to bring our portfolio of products to more than 2,000 independent veterinary practices.

 

“Our international business remained a key growth driver, with revenue increasing 20% year over year in the third quarter and 11% for the first three quarters of the year. We believe our expanding global footprint, together with continued investment in commercial partnerships and product adoption, provides a foundation for further growth.

 

“Development Services also continues to create incremental revenue opportunities by leveraging our existing engineering, development, and manufacturing capabilities, generating $1.1 million in third quarter; $4.5 million through the third quarter of 2026 and $7.6 million since its introduction in the second half of 2025.

 

“Alongside revenue growth, we maintained our focus on consistent and disciplined cost and cash management. Operating expenses declined 21%, or $2.5 million, compared to the prior year third quarter, while cash burn improved by $1.5M or 32% to $3.2M. This represents our lowest third quarter cash burn on record and second straight quarter under our historical run-rate of $4 million (adjusted for one-time events and acquisitions). Year-to-date cash burn has improved by $4.6 million, reflecting continued fiscal discipline while supporting top-line revenue growth.

 

“Our Therapeutic Devices segment, which makes up approximately two-thirds of our revenue, continued to deliver gross margins of 64% for the quarter. Consolidated gross margin remained strong at 61%, while affected by product mix, the ramp-up of new products, and inflationary pressures.

 

“With record third quarter revenue, continued progress in expanding our commercial reach, and a disciplined approach to spending, we believe we are well positioned to close the year strong. Our priorities remain focused on executing our growth strategy, improving our operating efficiency, and creating long-term shareholder value.” concluded Mr. Heaton.

2026 Third Quarter Financial Highlights

 

 

·

 Record Quarterly Revenue: Revenue increased 22% year-over-year to $9.9 million, marking the strongest third quarter on record and our second consecutive quarter with revenue exceeding $9 million.

 

 

 

 

·

 Broad-Based Commercial Performance: Compared to the third quarter of 2025, Diagnostic revenue increased 113%, Therapeutic Devices revenue increased 7%, and Consumables revenue increased 26%, alongside meaningful contributions from Development Services.

 

 

 

 

·

 Core Business Growth: Revenue excluding Development Services increased 18% year-over-year.

 

 

 

 

·

 Improved Cost Discipline: Operating expenses decreased 21%, or $2.5 million, compared to the third quarter of 2025.

 

 

 

 

·

 Reduced Cash Burn: Cash burn improved by $1.5 million, or 32%, to $3.2 million, representing Zomedica’s lowest third quarter cash burn since commercialization.

 

 

 

 

·

 Strong Liquidity: Cash, cash equivalents, and other qualifying liquidity components total $40.9 million as of September 30, 2026.

2026 Third Quarter and Year-To-Date Results Review

 

Revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $9.9 million, compared to $8.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of 22% or $1.8 million.

 

Revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $28.2 million, compared to $21.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, an increase of 31% or $6.6 million.

 

Revenue by Product Segment:

 

 

·

 Diagnostics, comprised of our TRUFORMA®, VETGuardian®, and TRUVIEW® products, generated revenue of $1.6 million, up 113% year-over-year for the quarter and up $4.0 million, or 89%, year-over-year for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

 

 

 

·

 Therapeutic Devices, comprised of our PulseVet®, Assisi®, and VETIGEL® products, generated revenue of $7.2 million, up 7% year-over-year for the quarter, and up $19.7 million, or 5%, year-over-year for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

 

 

 

·

 Development Services, which leverages the Company’s engineering, development, and manufacturing capabilities to support strategic opportunities in both animal and human health, generated revenue of $1.1 million and $4.5 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Revenue by Product Category:

 

 

·

 Consumable revenue, supported by increased adoption of our TRUFORMA® platform and utilization and continued strength in PulseVet® trode sales from both new installations and reorders, generated revenue of $6.8 million, up 26% year-over-year for the quarter and $18.9 million, up 25% year-over-year for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. We expect this opportunity to continue as our installed base grows.

 

 

 

 

·

 Capital revenue, comprised of PulseVet and VETGuardian® product sales, generated revenue of $2.0 million and $5.8 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

 

 

 

·

 Engineering Services, associated with our Development Services segment, generated revenues of $1.0 million and $3.2 million for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Gross Margin was 61% and 62% for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Total operating expenses were $9.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $12.0 million in 2025, representing an improvement of $2.5 million or 21%.

 

Total operating expenses were $30.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $93.7 million in 2025, which included impairment charges of $55.8 million triggered by the decline in the Company’s market capitalization, a function of the Company’s share price. There have been no such non-cash impairment charges in 2026.

 

Excluding these impairment charges, adjusted operating expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, were $30.0 million in 2026 compared to $37.9 million in 2025, representing a decrease of $7.9 million, or 21%.

 

Research and development expenses were $1.2 million (down $0.6 million or 33% year-over-year) and $3.4 million (down $2.1 million or 38% year-over-year) for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Selling and marketing expenses were $3.6 million (down $0.7 million or 18% year-over-year and $11.1 million (down $3.0 million or 21% year-over-year) for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

General and administrative expenses were $4.7 million (down $1.2 million or 20% year-over-year) and $15.5 million (down $2.8 million or 15% year-over-year) for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $3.2 million compared to $6.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $11.5 million compared to $77.3 million in 2025, which included the non-cash impairment expense discussed above.

 

*Non-GAAP EBITDA loss (which includes adjustments for stock compensation) for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $1.8 million compared to $3.9 million in 2025. Non-GAAP EBITDA loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $6.5 million compared to $71.4 million in 2025, which included the non-cash impairment expense discussed above.

 

*Adjusted Non-GAAP EBITDA loss (excluding the non-recurring and non-cash items noted above) for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $1.6 million compared to $3.9 million in 2025. Adjusted Non-GAAP EBITDA loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $6.3 million compared to $15.6 million in 2025, which included the non-cash impairment expense discussed above.

About Zomedica

 

Zomedica is a leading equine and companion animal healthcare company dedicated to improving animal health by providing veterinarians with innovative therapeutic and diagnostic solutions. Our gold standard PulseVet® shock wave system, which accelerates healing in musculoskeletal conditions, has transformed veterinary therapeutics. Our suite of products also includes the Assisi Loop® line of therapeutic devices and the TRUFORMA® diagnostic platform, the TRUVIEW® digital cytology system, the VetGuardian PLUS® Zero Touch® monitoring system and VETIGEL® hemostatic gel, all designed to empower veterinarians to provide top-tier care. In the aggregate, their total addressable market in the U.S. exceeds $2 billion. Headquartered in Michigan, Zomedica employs approximately 150 people and manufactures and distributes its products from its world-class facilities in Georgia and Minnesota. Zomedica grew revenue 17% in 2025 to $32 million, 31% through the first three quarters of 2026, and maintains a strong balance sheet with approximately $41 million in liquidity as of September 30, 2026. Zomedica is advancing its product offerings, leveraging strategic acquisitions, and expanding internationally as we work to enhance the quality of care for pets, increase pet parent satisfaction, and improve the workflow, cash flow and profitability of veterinary practices. For more information visit www.zomedica.com.
