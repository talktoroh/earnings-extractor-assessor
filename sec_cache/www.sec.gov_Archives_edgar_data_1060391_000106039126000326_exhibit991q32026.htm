PHOENIX (October 29, 2026) – Republic Services, Inc. (NYSE: RSG) today reported net income of $592 million, or $1.93 per diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, versus $550 million, or $1.76 per diluted share, for the comparable 2025 period. Excluding certain expenses and other items, on an adjusted basis, net income for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $595 million, or $1.94 per diluted share, versus $594 million, or $1.90 per diluted share, for the comparable 2025 period.

“We delivered strong third quarter results that underscored the resilience of our business model and the effectiveness of our strategy," said Jon Vander Ark, president and chief executive officer. "Positive growth trends across the business and improved performance in Environmental Solutions reinforce our confidence in the opportunities ahead. We remain focused on disciplined pricing, effective cost management and strategic investments that are designed to strengthen our competitive position and create long-term value for our customers and shareholders.”

Third-Quarter and Year-to-Date 2026 Highlights:

•Total revenue growth of 8.2 percent includes 4.8 percent organic growth from our recycling and waste business, 2.1 percent growth from our environmental solutions business, and 1.3 percent growth from acquisitions.

•Core price on total revenue increased revenue by 5.3 percent. Core price on related business revenue increased revenue by 6.3 percent, which consisted of 7.6 percent in the open market and 4.1 percent in the restricted portion of the business.

•Revenue growth from average yield on total revenue was 2.9 percent, and volume decreased revenue by 0.6 percent. Revenue growth from average yield on related business revenue was 3.5 percent, and volume decreased related business revenue by 0.7 percent.

•Net income was $592 million, or a margin of 13.0 percent.

•EPS was $1.93 per share, an increase of 9.7 percent over the prior year.

•Adjusted EPS, a non-GAAP measure, was $1.94 per share, an increase of 2.1 percent over the prior year.

•Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was $1.45 billion. Adjusted EBITDA margin, a non-GAAP measure, was 31.8 percent of revenue.

•Year-to-date cash invested in acquisitions was $1.12 billion.

•Year-to-date cash returned to shareholders was $1.43 billion, which included $848 million of share repurchases and $577 million of dividends paid.

•The Company's average recycled commodity price per ton sold at its recycling centers during the third quarter was $142. This represents an increase of $16 per ton over the prior year.

•Republic was recognized by several leading organizations during the quarter, including:
◦Great Place to Work certification for the 10th consecutive year
◦3BL's 2026 100 Best Corporate Citizens list

About Republic Services

Republic Services, Inc. is a leader in the environmental services industry. Through its subsidiaries, the Company provides customers with the most complete set of products and services, including recycling, solid waste, special waste, hazardous waste and field services. Republic’s industry-leading commitments to advance circularity and support decarbonization are helping deliver on its vision to partner with customers to create a more sustainable world. We believe that presenting EBITDA and EBITDA margin is useful to investors because they provide important information concerning our operating performance exclusive of certain non-cash and other costs. EBITDA and EBITDA margin demonstrate our ability to execute our financial strategy, which includes reinvesting in existing capital assets to ensure a high level of customer service, investing in capital assets to facilitate growth in our customer base and services provided, maintaining our investment grade credit ratings and minimizing debt, paying cash dividends, repurchasing our common stock, and maintaining and improving our market position through business optimization. Although depreciation, depletion, amortization and accretion are considered operating costs in accordance with U.S. GAAP, they represent the allocation of non-cash costs generally associated with long-lived assets acquired or constructed in prior years.
We believe that presenting adjusted EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA margin, adjusted EBITDA margin by business type, adjusted pre-tax income, adjusted tax impact, adjusted net income - Republic, and adjusted diluted earnings per share provide an understanding of operational activities before the financial impact of certain items. We use these measures, and believe investors will find them helpful, in understanding the ongoing performance of our operations separate from items that have a disproportionate impact on our results for a particular period. We have incurred comparable charges, costs and recoveries in prior periods, and similar types of adjustments can reasonably be expected to be recorded in future periods.
Restructuring charges. During the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, we incurred restructuring charges of $4 million and $10 million, respectively, and during the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, we incurred restructuring charges of $3 million and $13 million, respectively. The charges in these periods related primarily to the design and implementation of our new accounts receivable system.
Labor disruption. During the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, we experienced labor disruptions in certain isolated markets. The impact of these labor disruptions during both the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025 was $56 million, including $16 million of customer credits and $40 million of cost of operations.
Gain on business divestitures and impairments, net. During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, we recorded a net gain on business divestitures and impairments of $1 million.
