Enveric Biosciences Reports Financial Results for Third Quarter 2026 and Provides Corporate Update

 

EB-003 Development Highlights:

 

●Advancing final IND-enabling activities to support the planned EB-003 IND application
●Completed IND-enabling absorption and metabolic studies with favorable findings supporting EB-003’s potential for reduced drug-drug interactions
●Reported preclinical qEEG results demonstrating brain penetration and activity distinct from DMT, supporting evaluation as a potential biomarker
●Reported in vitro mechanistic studies, supporting EB-003’s predicted non-hallucinogenic profile

 

Corporate and Business Development Highlights:

 

●Secured U.S. patent allowance for next-generation, non-hallucinogenic mescaline derivatives in EVM401 Series
●Supported the FDA release of finalized guidance for clinical investigations of psychedelic and psychedelic-inspired therapeutics.

 

CAMBRIDGE, Mass., November 13, 2026 – Enveric Biosciences (NASDAQ: ENVB) (“Enveric” or the “Company”), a biotechnology company advancing novel neuroplastogenic small-molecule therapeutics to address psychiatric and neurological disorders, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a comprehensive business update.

 

CEO Commentary:

 

“Our focus during the third quarter was advancing final IND-enabling activities for EB-003 as we prepare for the planned IND submission and first-in-human Phase 1 clinical trial of our lead non-hallucinogenic, neuroplastogenic candidate,” said Joseph Tucker, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of Enveric. “The data generated during the quarter and in recent weeks strengthen the foundation for this next stage and reinforce the opportunity to develop EB-003 as a differentiated treatment for psychiatric and neurological disorders.”

 

Dr. Tucker continued: “Recently completed studies of EB-003 have provided important, complementary evidence demonstrating its differentiated profile and potential advantages. Independent assay platforms, including GloSensor™ and BRET, have consistently supported its predicted non-hallucinogenic profile, while our completed in vitro ADMET studies indicate favorable absorption and metabolic properties and a reduced potential for drug-drug interactions. The qEEG study adds functional evidence of brain penetration and a pattern of brain activity distinct from dimethyltryptamine (DMT). It also supports evaluating qEEG in human studies to connect drug exposure with brain activity and help guide dose selection.”

 

 

 

 

“As the FDA continues to define the regulatory framework for psychedelic-inspired therapies, we believe EB-003’s design has the potential to offer advantages in clinical development while supporting broader patient access and potential commercial adoption,” Dr. Tucker concluded. “By seeking to harness the therapeutic potential of neuroplasticity without requiring a hallucinogenic experience, our approach could support a simpler outpatient treatment model. The growing body of preclinical evidence reinforces our confidence in this opportunity as we complete the final IND-enabling activities and prepare to advance EB-003 toward first-in-human clinical development.”

 

THIRD QUARTER 2026 AND RECENT BUSINESS HIGHLIGHTS

 

Corporate, Product and Business Development Highlights:

 

EB-003 Development:

 

●Reported a preclinical qEEG study demonstrating functional evidence of central nervous system penetration and a brain activity profile distinct from the classic psychedelic DMT

 

○Results support use of qEEG as a potential pharmacodynamic biomarker in first-in-human development

 

●Announced positive results from recently completed IND-enabling in vitro Absorption, Distribution, Metabolism, Excretion and Toxicity (ADMET) studies for EB-003

 

●Reported findings from a highly sensitive GloSensor™ cAMP assay that provided additional evidence supporting EB-003’s predicted non-hallucinogenic profile

 

●Supported the release of finalized guidance from FDA for clinical investigations of psychedelic and psychedelic-inspired therapeutics

 

Intellectual Property:

 

●Secured New U.S. Patent Allowance for Next-Generation, Non-Hallucinogenic Mescaline Derivatives in EVM401 Series

 

THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS

 

Net loss attributable to stockholders was $2.5 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, including $0.2 million in net non-cash expense, with a basic and diluted loss per share of $0.57, as compared to a net loss attributable to stockholders of $3.4 million, including $0.1 million in net non-cash expense, with a basic and diluted loss per share of $10.81 for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The net loss attributable to stockholders for the quarter ended September 30, 2025 also included a $1.5 million deemed dividend on the inducement of warrants, which did not recur in the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company had cash-on-hand of $5.6 million as of September 30, 2026.

 

About Enveric Biosciences

 

Enveric Biosciences (NASDAQ: ENVB) is a biotechnology company focused on developing next-generation, small-molecule neuroplastogenic therapeutics that address unmet needs in psychiatric and neurological disorders. By leveraging a differentiated drug discovery platform and a growing library of protected chemical structures, Enveric is advancing a pipeline of novel compounds designed to promote neuroplasticity without hallucinogenic effects. Enveric’s lead candidate, EB-003, is the first known compound designed to selectively engage both 5-HT2A and 5-HT1B receptors with the potential to deliver fast-acting, durable antidepressant and anxiolytic effects with outpatient convenience.

 

For more information, please visit www.enveric.com.
