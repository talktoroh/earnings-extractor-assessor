Tvardi Therapeutics Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results and

Provides Business Update

HOUSTON, TX – November 13, 2026 – Tvardi Therapeutics, Inc. (“Tvardi” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: TVRD), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on the development of novel, oral, small molecule therapies targeting STAT3 to treat inflammatory and proliferative diseases, today reported its ﬁnancial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

Business and Clinical Program Updates:

TTI-109 – Ulcerative Colitis (UC)

·

Advancing development plans for TTI-109 in UC. Building on positive Phase 1 healthy volunteer results reported in July and the subsequent selection of UC as its initial target indication, Tvardi is preparing to advance TTI-109 into clinical development in UC, subject to clearance of our Investigational New Drug (IND) application and additional funding.

o

The Phase 1 results confirmed the prodrug design, with TTI-109 delivering TTI-101-equivalent exposure with improved tolerability and, across the active dose range, demonstrated reductions in cellular and humoral immune populations known to correlate with UC disease severity, providing pharmacodynamic evidence of STAT3 target engagement.

·

Published additional preclinical data supporting STAT3 inhibition in UC. New findings published in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences demonstrated that, in a preclinical model of chronic colitis, TTI-101 achieved colon tissue exposure approximately eightfold higher than in plasma, normalized disease-associated changes in colon gene expression, muted colitis-associated changes in the gut microbiome and prevented the formation of colon adenocarcinomas, a long-term consequence of chronic colitis.

o

The preclinical data highlights the potential of STAT3 inhibition to address immune dysregulation, inflammation and fibrosis that drive UC progression. These findings build on prior preclinical studies in which TTI-101 and TTI-109 significantly reduced disease activity across three acute colitis models representing distinct immune axes.

TTI-101 – Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC)

·

Advancing Phase 1b/2 REVERT LIVER CANCER study toward topline readout in early first quarter of 2027. The ongoing REVERT LIVER CANCER study is evaluating TTI-101 as monotherapy and in combination with established therapies in patients with HCC. The Company expects to report topline data early in the first quarter of 2027 to allow for complete cohort evaluation.

Imran Alibhai, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of Tvardi, stated, “We continue to make meaningful progress across our pipeline. Positive Phase 1 results for TTI-109 have strengthened our confidence in its potential as we prepare to advance this next-generation STAT3 inhibitor into clinical development in ulcerative colitis and potentially other inflammatory bowel diseases in 2027, subject to the availability of additional funding. In HCC, we remain encouraged by the previously reported interim results for TTI-101 and look forward to the topline readout in early first quarter of 2027, which will help inform our next steps.”

Key Upcoming Milestones

·

2027: Initiation of clinical trial of TTI-109 in UC, subject to IND clearance and additional funding

·

Early first quarter of 2027: TTI-101 Phase 1b/2 HCC topline data

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $4.5 million, compared to $3.6 million for the comparable period in 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher clinical costs associated with TTI-101.

General and administrative expenses were $1.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by lower professional fees.

Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $6.1 million, compared to a net loss of $5.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

Basic and diluted net loss per share attributable to common stockholders for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were both $(0.48), compared to basic and diluted net loss per share of $(0.59) for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

Cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, were $22.3 million, compared to cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments of $30.8 million as of December 31, 2025. Tvardi anticipates that its existing cash and cash equivalents will be sufficient to fund operations, as currently planned, into the third quarter of 2027. Advancing TTI-109 into UC and additional indications remain subject to additional funding and IND clearance.

About Tvardi Therapeutics

Tvardi is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on the development of novel, oral small molecule therapies targeting STAT3 to treat inflammatory and proliferative diseases with significant unmet need. STAT3 is a central mediator across critical signaling pathways that drive immune dysregulation, inflammation, and proliferation. STAT3 is also positioned at the intersection of many signaling pathways integral to the survival and immune evasion of cancer cells. The Company has completed a Phase 1 healthy volunteer study of TTI-109 and plans to initiate clinical trials of TTI-109 in ulcerative colitis pending IND clearance and the availability of additional funding. The Company is also conducting a Phase 1b/2 clinical trial of TTI-101 in hepatocellular carcinoma (NCT05440708). To learn more, please visit tvarditherapeutics.com or follow us on LinkedIn and X (Twitter).
