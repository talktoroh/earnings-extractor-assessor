Silence Therapeutics Highlights Recent Business Achievements and Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

12 November 2026

LONDON, Silence Therapeutics plc, (Nasdaq: SLN), a global clinical-stage company developing novel siRNA (short interfering RNA) therapies, today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on other recent business achievements.

Recent Business Highlights

 

  •  

On August 10, 2026, Silence reported positive topline results from its Phase 2 SANRECO trial of divesiran, an investigational siRNA therapy silencing TMPRSS6, in polycythemia vera, supporting a potentially best-in-class profile with once-every-three-month dosing.

 

  •  

SANRECO Phase 2 results have been accepted for oral presentation at the upcoming American Society of Hematology (ASH) 2026 Annual Meeting, being held December 12-15, 2026 in New Orleans, LA. You can find the presentation details here.

 

  •  

On October 19, 2026, Silence announced (press release) that it has entered into a definitive agreement to be acquired by Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

  •  

Cash Position: Cash and cash equivalents were $247.7 million as of September 30, 2026. This includes $201.3 million in gross proceeds from an upsized public offering announced on August 11, 2026.

 

  •  

R&D Expenses: Research and development (R&D) expenses were $9.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $20.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The $11.2 million decrease is mainly due to the zerlasiran Phase 3 readiness being completed in 2025 as well as reduced personnel costs.

 

  •  

G&A Expenses: General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $4.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The $0.9 million decrease is mainly due to an effort to reduce administrative costs.

 

  •  

Net Loss: Net loss was $12.4 million, or $0.08 basic and diluted net loss per share for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $21.0 million, or $0.15 basic and diluted net loss per share for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

 

  •  

Total outstanding shares were 186,609,073 ordinary shares (including shares in the form of American Depositary Shares) as of September 30, 2026.

About Silence Therapeutics

Silence Therapeutics is a global clinical-stage biotechnology company committed to transforming people’s lives by silencing diseases through precision engineered medicines created with proprietary siRNA (short interfering RNA) technology. Silence leverages its mRNAi GOLD™ platform to create innovative siRNA therapies designed to precisely target and silence genes that cause disease. The Company is advancing a growing pipeline of siRNA product candidates targeting areas of high unmet need across rare and common diseases where treatments are limited or inadequate. For more information, please visit https://www.silence-therapeutics.com/.
