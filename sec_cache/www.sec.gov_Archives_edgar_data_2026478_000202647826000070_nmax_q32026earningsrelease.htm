Newsmax Announces Third Quarter 2026
Financial Results

Company Reports Record Quarterly Revenues of $69.0 million, a 52.5% Year-Over-Year Increase

Company Reports Net Income of $15.2 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $19.0 million

Company Updates Full-Year 2026 Outlook

BOCA RATON, FL – November 12, 2026 – Newsmax Inc. (NYSE: NMAX) (“Newsmax” or the “Company”) today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Business and Operational Highlights

•Delivered third quarter audience reach of 22.5 million total viewers, including 9.1 million Adults 35-64, reinforcing Newsmax’s position as the fourth highest-rated cable news channel and #2 in cable news audience engagement among Adults 35-64.

•Grew total social media followers 13% year-over-year to 26.8 million as of September 30, 2026, further strengthening the Company’s multi-platform audience ecosystem.

•Broadened the availability of Newsmax with the launch on AFN Now, the streaming service of the American Forces Network, and additional carriage agreements with independent cable operators, while the Newsmax+ library grew to more than 700 licensed and original documentaries and specials.

•Advanced previously signed international brand license agreements toward launch, with Newsmax Ukraine going live on November 1, 2026 and Newsmax en Español expanding its streaming distribution in the United States, while 2026 international licensing revenues remain on track to increase 344% year-over-year to approximately $16 million, strengthening the Newsmax brand, which is available in more than 100 countries worldwide.











Management Commentary

“The third quarter was the strongest in our history as a public company. Setting aside the one-time revenue item, we delivered our fastest revenue growth since our listing and a second consecutive profitable quarter,” said Christopher Ruddy, Chief Executive Officer of Newsmax. “Engagement rose through the midterm campaign and that is an early indicator of what is ahead. We have positioned Newsmax on all platforms for all people, from cable and free streaming to Newsmax+, social and now AI, heading into what we expect to be the largest presidential cycle on record.”

Ruddy continued, “We are using this period to invest in programming, talent and content, while our affiliate agreements come up for renewal and our licensing partners carry the Newsmax brand into new markets in a capital-efficient way. The center-right audience remains under-served, with very few true alternatives, and we remain focused on delivering independent journalism and sustainable, long-term growth for our shareholders.”

“Our third quarter results reflect the operating leverage we are building across the business, with affiliate and licensing growth continuing to shift our revenue mix toward our highest-margin streams. Excluding the one-time revenue item, revenue grew 21.6% and Adjusted EBITDA was positive for a second consecutive quarter,” commented Darryle Burnham, Chief Financial Officer of Newsmax. “With a strong balance sheet, no debt and a disciplined approach to capital allocation, we will continue to invest in content, distribution and OTT initiatives that supports long-term growth and sustainable, long-term shareholder value.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:

•Newsmax reported total quarterly revenues of $69.0 million for the three-month period ended September 30, 2026, representing a 52.5% year-over-year increase. Total revenues include $14.0 million recognized in connection with a non-recurring service revenue termination payment of an advertising purchase agreement with a customer. Excluding this one-time revenue item, total revenues were a record $55.0 million, a 21.6% year-over-year increase and the highest quarterly revenues in the Company’s history, and advertising revenues were $28.1 million.

•Total broadcasting revenues grew 65.9% year-over-year to $60.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, or 27.6% to $46.7 million excluding the $14.0 million non-recurring revenue item. This was driven by growth in affiliate fee revenue, attributed to new contractual relationships and rate increases that took effect in late 2025 and 2026, and expanded international licensing agreements, with broadcasting advertising revenue up 3.1% year-over-year.






•Newsmax reported quarterly net income of $15.2 million, or $0.12 per share, as compared to a net loss of $(4.1) million, or $(0.03) per share, reported in the same quarter in the prior year. The improvement was primarily driven by higher total revenue, growth in high-margin affiliate fee and licensing revenue and lower legal expenses, partially offset by continued investment in programming and production.

•Quarterly Adjusted EBITDA was $19.0 million, an improvement of $20.0 million from $(0.9) million reported in the same quarter last year, primarily due to the $14.0 million one-time revenue item, growth in high-margin affiliate fee and licensing revenue and lower general and administrative expenses, partially offset by continued investment in programming, production and OTT initiatives. Excluding the one-time revenue item, Adjusted EBITDA was $5.0 million. See reconciliation of net income (loss) to Adjusted EBITDA below.

•The Company ended the quarter with $137.6 million in cash and short-term investments. Cash and cash equivalents were $26.7 million and short-term investments were $110.8 million.

The Company is maintaining its underlying full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $212 million to $216 million, which excludes the $14.0 million one-time revenue item recognized in the third quarter and represents 13% year-over-year growth at the midpoint of the range. Including the one-time revenue item, the Company’s full-year 2026 revenue guidance is $226 million to $230 million.

About Newsmax

Newsmax Inc. is listed on the NYSE (NMAX) and operates, through Newsmax Broadcasting LLC, one of the nation's leading news outlets, the Newsmax channel. The fourth highest-rated network is carried on all major pay TV providers. Newsmax's media properties reach more than 50 million Americans regularly through Newsmax TV, the Newsmax App, its popular website Newsmax.com, and publications such as Newsmax Magazine. Through its social media accounts, Newsmax reaches over 26 million combined followers. Reuters Institute says Newsmax is one of the top U.S. news brands and Forbes has called Newsmax "a news powerhouse."

USE AND DEFINITION OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

This press release contains a financial measure that has not been prepared in accordance with United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles ("U.S. GAAP"). This financial measure is Adjusted EBITDA.

Non-GAAP financial measures are used to supplement the financial information presented on a U.S. GAAP basis and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the relevant U.S. GAAP measures and should be read in conjunction with information presented on a U.S. GAAP basis. Because not all companies use identical calculations, our presentation of Non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies.

Adjusted EBITDA1 is defined as revenues less cost of revenues and general and administrative expenses and does not include depreciation, amortization related to the incremental costs to obtain a contract, interest expense, net, impairment charges, unrealized gains (losses) on marketable securities and crypto assets, stock-based compensation, other corporate matters (consisting primarily of certain litigation expenses, and related fees, for specific legal proceedings that the Company has determined are infrequent and unusual in terms of their magnitude), other, net, and income tax expense.

You are encouraged to evaluate each adjustment used in calculating our non-GAAP financial measure and the reasons we consider our non-GAAP financial measure appropriate for supplemental analysis. In evaluating our non-GAAP financial measure, you should be aware that in the future we may incur expenses similar to the adjustments in our presentation. Our non-GAAP financial measure has limitations as an analytical tool, and you should not consider this measure in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our results as reported under GAAP. Our presentation of our non-GAAP financial measure should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. Our non-GAAP financial measure may not be comparable to other companies.
