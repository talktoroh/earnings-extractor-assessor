Business First Bancshares, Inc. Announces Financial Results for Q3 2026
Baton Rouge, La. (October 26, 2026) – Business First Bancshares, Inc. (NASDAQ: BFST) (Business First), parent company of b1BANK, today announced its unaudited results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Business First reported net income available to common shareholders of $23.2 million or $0.71 per diluted common share, increases of $0.4 million and $0.01, respectively, compared to the linked quarter. On a non-GAAP basis, core net income available to common shareholders for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, which excludes certain income and expenses, was $26.0 million or $0.79 per diluted common share, increases of $2.7 million and $0.08 from the linked quarter.
“Our third quarter showed what disciplined execution can deliver,” said Jude Melville, chairman, president, and CEO of Business First. “We completed the conversion of Progressive Bank, and I’m grateful to our North Louisiana teammates and clients for helping make it a seamless transition. We grew loans and deposits at a balanced pace appropriate to our level of capital strength, and we did so while expanding our net interest margin by 12 basis points and keeping non-interest expenses in check. The result was higher earnings and stronger returns. Our team earned these results through disciplined execution, and we’ll keep that same focus as we continue to invest in the growing number of opportunities we’re seeing across our diversified footprint.”

On Thursday, October 22, 2026, Business First’s board of directors declared a quarterly preferred dividend in the amount of $18.75 per share, which is the full quarterly dividend of 1.875% based on the per annum rate of 7.50%. Additionally, the board of directors declared a quarterly common dividend based upon financial performance for the third quarter in the amount of $0.15 per share of common stock. The preferred and common dividends will be paid on November 30, 2026, or as soon thereafter as practicable, to the shareholders of record as of November 15, 2026.

Quarterly Highlights
• Improving Core Performance. Return to common shareholders on average assets, on an annualized basis, was 1.04% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, or 1.17% on a non-GAAP basis, compared to 1.03% or 1.05% on a non-GAAP basis for the linked quarter. Return to common shareholders on average equity on an annualized basis, was 9.75% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, or 10.93% on a non-GAAP basis, compared to 9.83%, or 10.05% on a non-GAAP basis for the linked quarter.
• Margin Expansion. Net interest margin expanded 13 basis points (bps) to 3.86% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, or 12 bps to 3.80% on a non-GAAP basis, respectively, compared to 3.73% or 3.68%, respectively, for the linked quarter. The expansion was driven by improvement in book yield on loans and a reduction in the overall cost of funding. The reduction in overall cost of funding was driven by decreases of $65.9 million in brokered deposits and $107.8 million in borrowings while core deposit costs remained flat.
• Loan and Deposit Growth. Loans grew by $121.4 million or 7.23%, annualized and deposits grew by $141.2 million or 7.74% annualized, led by our Houston, Texas and Southwest Louisiana markets.
• Capital and Share Repurchases. During the third quarter, as part of a previously announced stock repurchase program, Business First repurchased 204,160 shares, with a market value of $6.3 million, at a weighted average price of $30.81 per share. Common equity to total assets decreased from 10.52% to 10.39%. Business First has repurchased a total of 630,618 common shares under the existing stock repurchase program, since October 2025, or $17.6 million at a weighted average repurchase price per share of $27.88.
• Progressive Conversion. In August 2026, Business First successfully converted Progressive Bank’s core systems onto its platform.
Statement of Financial Condition
Loans
Loans held for investment increased $121.4 million or 1.82%, 7.23% annualized, compared to the linked quarter. The commercial real estate and construction portfolios increased $72.7 million and $80.7 million, respectively, compared to the linked quarter. The residential real estate portfolio decreased $30.6 million compared to the linked quarter.

Credit Quality
Loans over 30 days past due at September 30, 2026 were $32.5 million or 0.48% of total loans, which was generally stable from the linked quarter. The ratio of nonperforming loans compared to loans held for investment decreased 2 bps to 1.24% at September 30, 2026, while the ratio of nonperforming assets compared to total assets also decreased by 1 bps to 1.22% compared to the linked quarter. Net charge-offs to average quarterly total loans increased to 8 bps for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, up from 4 bps in the linked quarter mainly attributable to one previously fully reserved commercial relationship.
Securities
The securities portfolio decreased $54.4 million or 5.23%, from the linked quarter, impacted by $24.2 million in negative pre-tax fair value adjustments and the remainder of the decrease attributed to maturities and paydowns. The securities portfolio, based on estimated fair value, represented 11.02% of total assets as of September 30, 2026.
Deposits
Deposits as of September 30, 2026 increased by $141.2 million or 1.95%, 7.74% annualized, compared to the linked quarter. During the third quarter, interest-bearing deposits increased $119.1 million or 2.11% and noninterest-bearing deposits increased $22.1 million or 1.40%. The increase in interest-bearing deposits was attributable to $180.1 million in commercial money market accounts, partially offset by a decrease of $65.9 million in brokered deposits. Average interest-bearing deposits decreased $9.5 million or 0.16%, and noninterest-bearing deposits increased $54.9 million or 3.62% from the linked quarter.
Borrowings
Borrowings decreased $107.8 million or 18.25%, from the linked quarter due primarily to decreases in short-term Federal Home Loan Bank advances.
Shareholders’ Equity
On a non-GAAP basis, tangible book value per common share increased from $23.61 at the linked quarter to $23.71 at September 30, 2026, 0.42% or 1.66% annualized, despite the negative pre-tax fair value adjustments. Shareholders' equity decreased $6.1 million or 0.60% compared to the linked quarter. Accumulated other comprehensive loss increased from $35.8 million to $54.9 million or 53.29%, during the quarter due to after-tax fair value adjustments in the securities portfolio. Book value per common share decreased to $28.77 at September 30, 2026, compared to $28.79 from the linked quarter.

Results of Operations
Net Interest Income
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, net interest income totaled $80.7 million, compared to $77.8 million from the linked quarter. Loan yields increased 11 bps to 6.74% compared to 6.63% from the linked quarter and interest-bearing asset yields increased 8 bps to 6.10% compared to 6.02% from the linked quarter. Net interest margin and net interest spread were 3.86% and 3.09% compared to 3.73% and 2.99% for the linked quarter. The overall cost of funds, which included noninterest-bearing deposits, decreased 4 bps from 2.44% to 2.40% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Non-GAAP net interest income (excluding loan discount accretion of $1.2 million) totaled $79.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $76.8 million (excluding loan discount accretion of $1.0 million) for the linked quarter. Non-GAAP net interest margin and net interest spread (excluding loan discount accretion of $1.2 million) were 3.80% and 3.04%, respectively, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to 3.68% and 2.94% (excluding loan discount accretion of $1.0 million) for the linked quarter.
Provision for Credit Losses
During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Business First recorded a provision for credit losses of $1.9 million, compared to $2.0 million from the linked quarter. At September 30, 2026, the ratio of allowance for credit losses to loans held for investment was 0.95%, compared to 1.02% for the linked quarter.
Other Income
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, other income decreased $1.5 million or 10.42%, compared to the linked quarter. The decrease was largely attributable to a $0.8 million decrease in gain on sales of loans and the linked quarter’s $0.5 million gain on the redemption of debt.
Other Expenses
For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, other expenses increased $0.9 million or 1.58% compared to the linked quarter. The increase was largely attributable to increases of $1.8 million in occupancy and equipment expense and $1.1 million in merger and conversion-related expenses, partially offset by decreases of $0.5 million in each in advertising and promotions expense and salaries and employee benefits, $0.4 million in legal and other professional fees and $0.3 million in regulatory assessments. The increase in occupancy and equipment expense was largely attributable to a lease termination fee associated with better alignment of legacy b1BANK and Progressive corporate facilities.
