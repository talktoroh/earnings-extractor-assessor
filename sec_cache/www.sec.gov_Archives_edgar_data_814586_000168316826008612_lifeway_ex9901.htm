Lifeway Foods® Reports Record High Quarterly Net Sales, Extends Kefir Category Leadership in Third Quarter 2026

 

28th consecutive quarter of year-over-year net sales growth; year-to-date 2026 net sales up 25.8%

 

 

Morton Grove, IL — November 12, 2026 — Lifeway Foods, Inc. (Nasdaq: LWAY) (“Lifeway” or “the Company”), the leading U.S. supplier of kefir and fermented probiotic foods, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Lifeway delivered another record breaking quarter with net sales of $67.8 million, extending our streak of topline growth to 28 consecutive quarters,” said Julie Smolyansky, President and Chief Executive Officer of Lifeway Foods. “The consistent, volume-led growth we are seeing for our authentic Lifeway Kefir and Lifeway Farmer Cheese is extraordinary, reflecting the deep trust we have established with our growing consumer base and the ongoing commitment of our retailer partners. Our performance also reflects the rising demand for fermented dairy, as consumers increasingly seek functional, high quality-protein offerings and retailers identify ways to dedicate more shelf space to products like ours. With 7 years of uninterrupted growth and decades spent building this category, Lifeway is uniquely positioned to meet the moment and capitalize on this demand.”

 

Ms. Smolyansky continued, “Profitability this quarter continued to reflect an elevated input cost environment, and while we expect the cost pressure to persist in the fourth quarter, our outlook for 2027 has not changed. We expect the planned price increases  across our portfolio to take effect with distributors and retailers on a rolling basis through the end of the year and into the first quarter of 2027. With our pricing actions and the productivity gains we anticipate from our capacity investments, we expect gross margin expansion and improved profitability in 2027.   The scale-up of our Waukesha facility, scheduled for completion in early 2027, will eliminate capacity challenges we have encountered and allow us to serve the massive level of demand in front of us. We are well on pace to shatter our annual net sales record in 2026, and look forward to extending our category leadership and building on this momentum as we look ahead to 2027.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

·Net Sales: $67.8 million, the highest quarterly net sales in Company history, up 18.6% year-over-year, primarily driven by higher volumes of the Company’s branded drinkable kefir.
·Gross Profit Margin: 18.0% , compared to 28.7% in the prior-year period, primarily reflecting elevated input costs including milk, resin-based packaging, ingredients, and transportation costs.
·Selling, general and administrative expenses were 16.2% of net sales, compared to 19.6% last year, reflecting continued investment in marketing and brand awareness.
·Net Income: $0.3 million, or $0.02 per basic and diluted common share, compared to net income of $3.5 million, or $0.23 per basic and diluted common share, in the prior-year period.

Year-to-Date 2026 Highlights

 

·Net Sales: $197.7 million, up 25.8% year-over-year.
·Gross Profit Margin: 21.5%, compared to 27.3% in the prior-year period.
·Selling, general and administrative expenses were 17.2% of net sales, compared to 19.1% last year,
·Net Income: $5.1 million, or $0.33 per basic and diluted common share, compared to net income of $11.3 million, or $0.75 per basic and $0.74 per diluted common share, in the prior-year period.

 

Historic Growth Record

 

·28 consecutive quarters of year-over-year net sales growth, reflecting seven years of uninterrupted quarterly growth.
·Four consecutive years of record annual net sales from 2022 through 2025, increasing from $141.6 million to $212.5 million – a cumulative increase of 50.1%.
·Year-to-date net sales equaled approximately 93% of full-year 2025 net sales, with the Company well on pace to set a record high in annual net sales in 2026, far exceeding the high-bar set in 2025.

 

Industry Recognition

 

In October, Lifeway was named to Fortune’s 2026 list of the 100 Fastest-Growing Companies, ranking No. 45, reflecting the Company’s sustained, volume-led growth and the strength of consumer demand for kefir and other cultured dairy products. This inclusion places Lifeway among an elite group of publicly traded companies that have delivered notably strong financial performance. This follows the induction of President and Chief Executive Officer Julie Smolyansky into the Specialty Food Association Hall of Fame this summer, in recognition of her leadership in transforming kefir from a niche product into a mainstream functional-food category.

About Lifeway Foods, Inc.

Lifeway Foods, Inc. is the brand that introduced kefir to generations of American consumers. For 40 years, Lifeway has defined authentic, real kefir in the U.S. market while building the expertise, consumer trust and scale that support its category leadership. The Company has been recognized as one of America’s Growth Leaders by TIME, as Dairy Foods’ Processor of the Year 2025, as one of Forbes’ Best Small Companies and on Inc.’s 2025 Best in Business list in the Best Challenger Brands category. In addition to its drinkable kefir portfolio, Lifeway produces Farmer Cheese – a 40-year legacy line now expanding to mass retail – Kefir Butter, Muscle Mates™, a variety of cheeses and ProBugs® for kids. Lifeway products are sold across the United States, Mexico, the United Arab Emirates, Central America and the Caribbean. Learn more at lifewayfoods.com
