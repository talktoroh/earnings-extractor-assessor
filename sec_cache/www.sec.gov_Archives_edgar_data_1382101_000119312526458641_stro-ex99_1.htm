Sutro Biopharma Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Highlights

– STRO-004, TF-targeting ADC, Phase 1 STRIVE-01 study advancing; next clinical update and initiation of expansion cohorts planned first half of 2027 –

– STRO-006, ITGB6-targeting ADC, enters clinic with first patient dosed; initial update expected second half of 2027 –

– STRO-227, Sutro’s PTK7-targeting dual-payload ADC, advancing toward clinical development early 2027 –

– Astellas dosed first patient in second dual-payload iADC program, triggering milestone payment to Sutro –

– Strong balance sheet with $142.2 million in cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, expected to support operations into first half of 2028 –

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., November 12, 2026 -- Sutro Biopharma, Inc. (Sutro or the Company) (NASDAQ: STRO), a clinical-stage oncology company pioneering site-specific and novel-format antibody drug conjugates (ADCs), today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and recent business highlights.

“Over the past year, we have delivered on our commitment to advance our next-generation ADCs with two programs, STRO-004 and STRO-006, entering the clinic in less than a year, and our dual-payload ADC, STRO-227, expected to follow in early 2027,” said Jane Chung, Chief Executive Officer of Sutro. “Early data from STRO-004 give us confidence in its potential to deliver meaningful clinical benefit with our proprietary ADC platform. In addition, our collaboration partner Astellas has initiated patient dosing with a second dual-payload immunostimulatory ADC, further highlighting our strong execution and ability to deliver differentiated, novel dual-payload ADCs. Together these advances reflect our proficiency in translating platform innovation into a growing pipeline of therapies designed to improve patient outcomes and create long-term value.”

 

Sutro Pipeline

STRO-004: Sutro’s potential best-in-class Tissue Factor (TF)-targeting DAR8 exatecan ADC is being evaluated in the ongoing Phase 1 STRIVE-01 study in patients with advanced solid tumors. As previously reported in August 2026, initial dose escalation in 49 heavily pretreated patients demonstrated encouraging early clinical activity, including confirmed and ongoing unconfirmed partial responses across three tumor types in RECIST-evaluable patients, alongside favorable

 

 

 

 

 

tolerability and a differentiated pharmacokinetic profile. Dose optimization continues in backfill cohorts of selected indications. The Company plans to provide its next STRIVE-01 study update and initiate expansion cohorts in select indications in the first half of 2027.

STRO-006: Sutro’s next-generation, highly selective integrin β6 (ITGB6)-targeting ADC with a DAR8 exatecan payload, is designed for the treatment of multiple solid tumors. In October 2026, the Company announced the first patient dosed in a Phase 1, open-label, multicenter study evaluating the safety, tolerability, pharmacokinetics and preliminary antitumor activity of STRO-006 as a monotherapy in adults with advanced or metastatic solid tumors. The dose-escalation portion will enroll patients with ITGB6-expressing tumor types, including head and neck cancer, non-small cell lung cancer, esophageal/gastric cancer, colorectal cancer, endometrial cancer, urothelial cancer and breast cancer, with dosing beginning at 1.5 mg/kg. The Company anticipates an initial Phase 1 data update in the second half of 2027.

STRO-227: Sutro’s DAR10 dual-payload ADC targeting PTK7, consists of MMAE (DAR2) and exatecan (DAR8) payloads to enable complementary mechanisms of action within a single molecule. The program is advancing toward initiation of a Phase 1 clinical trial in early 2027 and is a key component of Sutro’s strategy to expand its portfolio of ADCs designed for the potential to widen the therapeutic index and overcome resistance.

Next-Generation ADC Collaborations

Astellas: Two programs are advancing in the clinic under Sutro’s collaboration with Astellas focused on dual-payload immunostimulatory ADCs (iADCs). The first program, which targets TROP2, continues to actively dose patients.

The second program, targeting B7-H3, began dosing patients, resulting in a $10 million milestone payment received by Sutro in October 2026.

Recent Scientific Presentations

At the 17th Annual World ADC Conference held October 12–15, 2026, Sutro presented initial STRO-004 clinical safety and pharmacokinetic findings in a plenary session, along with new preclinical updates on STRO-006, STRO-227 and novel payload mechanisms, demonstrating the potential of the Company’s platform to deliver next-generation ADCs with a wider therapeutic index and the potential to overcome resistance to single-payload ADCs. The presentations are available in the Scientific Publications section of Sutro Biopharma’s website at www.sutrobio.com.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

Cash, Cash Equivalents and Marketable Securities

As of September 30, 2026, Sutro had cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities of $142.2 million, as compared to $164.3 million as of June 30, 2026. Based on its current operating plan, the Company expects its cash runway to support operations into the first half of 2028.

Revenue

Revenue was $9.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as compared to $9.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, with the 2026 amount related principally to the Astellas collaboration.

Research & Development (R&D) and General & Administrative (G&A) Expenses

Total R&D and G&A expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $31.0 million, as compared to $48.6 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

About Sutro Biopharma

Sutro Biopharma, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing a next-generation antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) platform designed to deliver single- and dual-payload ADCs that enable meaningful breakthroughs for patients with cancer. By fully optimizing the antibody, linker, and payload, Sutro’s cell-free platform produces ADCs that are engineered to improve drug exposure, reduce side effects, and expand the range of treatable tumor types. With unique capabilities in dual-payload ADCs, Sutro aims to overcome treatment resistance and redefine what’s possible in cancer therapy. The Company’s pipeline of single- and dual-payload ADCs targets large oncology markets with limited treatment options and significant need for improved therapies.
