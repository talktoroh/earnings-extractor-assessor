PRESS RELEASE

 

Sono Group N.V. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results: Swings to Net Income in First Full Quarter as Only a Bitcoin Treasury Company

 

Q3 net income of $0.6 million versus a $1.4 million loss in Q3 2025, driven principally by Bitcoin treasury income and non-cash fair-value gains; G&A down 24%; shareholders to vote on proposed move to Delaware on December 11, 2026

 

LAND O’LAKES, FL, November 10, 2026 – Sono Group N.V. (Nasdaq: SSM) (hereafter referred to as “Sono” or the “Company”) today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and the first nine months of 2026.

 

The third quarter was Sono’s first full quarter following the May 4, 2026 sale of its legacy solar business, and the first in which its results reflect its Bitcoin treasury strategy alone, which centers on holding Bitcoin and generating option premium income through an institutional covered-call framework with Blockchain.com. Net income for the quarter was $0.6 million, compared with a net loss of $1.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Q3 & Year-to-Date 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Net income: Net income of $0.6 million in Q3 2026, compared with a net loss of $1.4 million in Q3 2025. Income from operations was $0.1 million, compared with an operating loss of $0.8 million in Q3 2025. Basic and diluted net income per share were $0.32 and $0.00, respectively, with diluted net income per share reflecting the dilutive effect of Sono’s outstanding preferred shares and convertible debentures on an if-converted basis. For the first nine months of 2026, the Company reported a net loss of $5.1 million, compared with net income of $7.4 million in the prior-year period, which included a $12.4 million fair-value gain on predecessor convertible notes.

●Bitcoin treasury income: Q3 digital asset treasury income, net, was $0.7 million, consisting of a $1.5 million fair-value gain on Bitcoin holdings and $0.2 million of covered-call option income, net, partially offset by $1.0 million of losses on written covered calls exercised in the money and settled by delivery of Bitcoin. Because changes in the fair value of Bitcoin are recognized in earnings each period, results will fluctuate with the price of Bitcoin, and the Q3 fair-value gain is not necessarily indicative of future results. At September 30, 2026, the Company held 53.40 Bitcoin with a fair value of $4.4 million, all pledged as collateral to Blockchain.com to secure written covered call options.

 

●Other income and expense: Q3 net income included a non-cash gain of $1.1 million from the remeasurement of embedded conversion derivative liabilities associated with the Company’s convertible debentures, and was reduced by $0.6 million of interest expense on the convertible debentures, including non-cash amortization of debt discount.

 

●Leaner cost base: General and administrative expenses declined 24% to $0.6 million, from $0.8 million in Q3 2025. Following the sale of Sono Motors GmbH on May 4, 2026, there was no loss from discontinued operations in the quarter, compared with $1.1 million in Q3 2025.

 

Business Updates and Outlook

 

Proposed redomiciliation to Delaware will go to a shareholder vote in December 2026. On October 16, 2026, the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) declared effective the amended registration statement on Form S-4 relating to the proposed change of the Company’s legal seat from the Netherlands, via Luxembourg, to the State of Delaware (the “Redomiciliation Transaction”). The management board and supervisory board believe the redomiciliation would simplify Sono’s corporate structure and reporting, reduce the cost and complexity of maintaining a non-U.S. parent company, align its corporate domicile with the U.S. base of its board members and most of its executive leadership, and bring the Company under Delaware’s well-established corporate governance framework. The Redomiciliation Transaction is structured to preserve shareholders’ existing economic and voting interests, and, if the Redomiciliation Transaction is completed, Sono expects the Class A common stock of the continuing Delaware entity to continue trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the symbol “SSM,” subject to the satisfaction of applicable Nasdaq requirements. Completion remains subject to shareholder approval at an extraordinary general meeting of Sono’s shareholders to be held on December 11, 2026 (the “Special Meeting”) for shareholders of record as of November 13, 2026; the proposals to be voted on, the boards’ recommendations and voting instructions are described in the definitive proxy statement/prospectus, dated October 16, 2026, filed by Sono Group S.à r.l. with the SEC.

Sports One letter of intent. On August 31, 2026, Sono and Sports One announced a non-binding letter of intent for a proposed business combination. Under the letter of intent, Sports One’s existing equityholders would own a super-majority of the combined public company, which is expected to be renamed “Sports One.” Sports One was formed in 2026 to operate a sports intelligence business and intends to acquire and hold minority interests in professional sports teams, with a primary focus on the NBA, NFL, MLB and NHL franchises, subject to applicable league and team approvals. Concurrently with the entry into the letter of intent, a group of investors, including investors affiliated with Sports One, purchased 283,500 ordinary shares, representing 19.9% of the Company’s then-outstanding ordinary shares, in a registered direct offering at market price, with no discount and no warrant coverage. Each investor entered into a 180-day lock-up agreement with Sports One. As of the date of this release, the parties have not entered into a definitive agreement, and there can be no assurance that they will do so or that the proposed business combination will be completed on the terms or within the timeframe currently contemplated, or at all. Completion of the proposed business combination with Sports One is subject to, among other things, the negotiation and execution of definitive documentation, any required regulatory review, the receipt of shareholder approval (which would be sought separately and not at the December 11, 2026 Special Meeting), and the completion of the Redomiciliation Transaction.

 

Kevin McGurn, Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer, said: “Our first full quarter as a pure-play Bitcoin treasury company following the sale of our legacy solar business delivered net income on a leaner cost base compared to Q3 2025, with our treasury focused entirely on Bitcoin. We will keep executing our treasury strategy with discipline as we take the next steps, including the proposed redomiciliation to Delaware, which we believe would simplify our corporate structure and better position Sono to pursue future strategic opportunities.”

As disclosed in the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, management has concluded that there is substantial doubt about the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern. At September 30, 2026, the Company had cash of $0.5 million and Bitcoin with a fair value of $4.4 million, and $5.05 million principal amount of convertible debentures outstanding, maturing between January and April 2027. Management plans to address the Company’s liquidity needs through further debt or equity financings, sales of a portion of its Bitcoin holdings and premium income from its covered-call program.

 

The full unaudited quarterly report on Form 10-Q for the period ended September 30, 2026, is available on the Company’s investor relations website at ir.sonomotors.com and filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission.

ABOUT SONO GROUP N.V.

 

Sono Group N.V. (Nasdaq: SSM) is a Netherlands-incorporated company listed on the Nasdaq Capital Market, currently operating as a digital asset treasury company. The Company's treasury strategy is centered on the acquisition of Bitcoin and the generation of structured yield through an institutional covered-call approach under an ISDA Master Agreement framework. For more information about Sono Group N.V., visit sonogroupnv.com. Follow us on social media: LinkedIn and X.
