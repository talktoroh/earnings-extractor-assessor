S&P Global Reports Third Quarter Results; Announces Strategic Review for Capital IQ
and Associated Data Businesses

New York, NY, October 27, 2026 – S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI) today reported third quarter results. This earnings release and supplemental materials are available at http://investor.spglobal.com/Quarterly-Earnings.
The Company reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $3.678 billion, an increase of 7% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter GAAP net income increased 20% to $1.298 billion and GAAP diluted earnings per share increased 26% to $4.45. Adjusted net income for the third quarter increased 4% to $1.337 billion and adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 9% to $4.58. Higher GAAP net income was driven by growth in operating profit, including the benefit from a gain on disposition in Energy. Higher adjusted net income was driven primarily by growth in adjusted operating profit, partially offset by higher taxes in the quarter.
The Company has initiated a strategic review of alternatives, including potential separation, for Capital IQ and associated data businesses, which constitute the majority of Kensho Data & Platforms within Market Intelligence. While the exact perimeter remains under review, the Company expects to retain RatingsDirect, RatingsXpress, and Enterprise Solutions. Capital IQ and the associated data businesses are well positioned to grow and thrive, and we believe separate ownership could provide dedicated investment and focus, positioning these businesses to accelerate innovation and growth.
•The Company reported revenue of $3.678 billion, which increased 7% year over year.

•Operating margin increased 800 basis points and adjusted operating margin increased 60 basis points, driving 26% growth in diluted EPS and 9% growth in adjusted diluted EPS, respectively, year over year.

•In the third quarter, the Company executed $2.6 billion in share repurchases, and has repurchased more than $4 billion in shares year-to-date. The Company continues to expect to repurchase more than $7 billion in shares in total in 2026.

•2026 guidance calls for revenue growth of 7.4% to 8.4%, and organic constant currency growth of 7.5% to 8.5%.

"S&P Global delivered a very strong quarter. Increasing demand for our benchmarks and unique intellectual property demonstrates our leadership in credit, equity, and commodities markets.
Our focus on new high-growth adjacencies and our innovation are resonating deeply with customers around the world.
The businesses that are part of the strategic review are strong and have significant opportunities ahead of them as technology changes how customers access, analyze, and use information. All financial information presented in this release reflects the Company’s results on a continuing operations basis, unless otherwise stated, and prior periods have been recast accordingly to conform to this presentation. Division results presented in this release also reflect a recast of the Company’s division financial information for all periods presented, including inter-segment adjustments and changes to expense allocation methodologies as outlined in our press release dated July 6, 2026.

Total revenue and organic constant currency revenue are now expected to be higher than the previous guidance ranges of 5.9% to 7.9% and 6.0% to 8.0%, respectively, both driven by higher expected revenue from the Ratings and Indices divisions.
