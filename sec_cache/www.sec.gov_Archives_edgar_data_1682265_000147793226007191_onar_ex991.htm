ONAR Issues Letter to Stockholders: Pro Forma Nine-Month Revenue of $13.5 Million With Advertise Purple, More Than Four Times the $3.1 Million Reported

 

The third quarter Form 10-Q includes two days of results from the Company’s largest acquisition to date and all of the debt from buying it. $11.0 million of that debt would convert to equity upon a listing on a senior exchange, and new capital was funded at a $25 million pre-money valuation with a fixed conversion price

 

Miami, FL, November 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- ONAR Holding Corporation (OTCID: ONAR) (“ONAR” or the “Company”), an AI-powered marketing platform, today filed its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and issued the following letter to stockholders from Chief Executive Officer Claude Zdanow.

 

Dear Fellow Stockholders,

 

In July, I gave you four priorities and said we would report on each one as it moved from plan to fact. Four months later, three of them are fact: the acquisition, the financing that paid for it, and the exchange of certain of our lenders’ older debt into the same convertible notes issued in the financing. The fourth, a listing of our common stock on a national securities exchange (a “listing”), is where the work is now.

What we bought. Advertise Purple LLC (“Advertise Purple”), a leader in affiliate and partnership marketing and a business more than three times our size by revenue.

What the company was valued at. $25 million pre-money and before any debt converts, which implies about $36 million to $40 million for the combined company once the debt converts. One fixed price, $0.038576 per share. Holders of our existing preferred stock intend to exchange into the same class.

What we bought

 

On September 29 we acquired Advertise Purple, a business more than three times our size by revenue. Advertise Purple is a leader in affiliate and partnership marketing, one of the fastest-growing channels in digital marketing and one in which clients pay primarily for measured outcomes. It manages performance-based programs for a roster of established, well-recognized brands. It fits what ONAR is: an AI-powered marketing platform that acquires specialist marketing companies and runs them on shared technology, so that growth-stage and middle-market brands sell more of their products and services online. Together, our operating companies now serve approximately 400 clients. Cortex, our attribution platform, began billing subscription clients in July and has completed its first full quarter of recurring billings. That revenue is not yet material, but it is the first recurring technology revenue ONAR Labs has generated. With Advertise Purple we also acquired Bloom, its affiliate performance data platform.

With Advertise Purple for the full period, pro forma nine-month revenue would have been $13.5 million, more than four times the $3.1 million we reported. Those figures combine two companies for a period before we owned one of them, and they are not a forecast. They do not include the interest on the acquisition debt, and they do not include any cost savings or synergies from combining the two companies. ONAR without the acquisition of Advertise Purple was already improving results: reported revenue grew 68% for the nine months, general and administrative expense fell 55%, and the loss from operations narrowed by 60%.

The cash

 

The cash picture is much better than the reported loss. For the nine months we reported a net loss of $3.61 million, but the business used only $0.59 million of cash in operations. Nearly three quarters of the loss was interest expense, and most of that interest was not paid in cash.

 

What the company was valued at

 

The investors in our September 28, 2026 financings, which funded the Advertise Purple acquisition, priced ONAR at $25 million pre-money and before any debt converts. Upon a listing, their convertible notes and the convertible notes our lenders exchanged older debt into become one class of preferred stock, Series 1. The Series 1 Preferred Stock converts into common stock at one fixed price, $0.038576 per share, which does not move with the market. On those terms, the equity after a listing would be valued at about $36 million once those notes convert, and about $40 million if the senior notes, which can convert on the same terms, convert as well. The $25 million is the value before any of that debt becomes equity. The $36 million to $40 million is the implied value of the combined company, including Advertise Purple, after the conversion of debt. The holders of our existing Series B, C and E preferred stock, including holders who also invested in the September 28 financings, intend to exchange into Series 1 Preferred Stock at the time of the listing, and the financing documents anticipate that exchange. However, definitive agreements regarding the preferred exchange are not yet signed and so it is not assured.

 

The $25 million valuation and the conversion terms are what the investors in the September 28 financings agreed to in their purchase agreements. That valuation is not the market value of our stock or a prediction of it, and conversion means more shares outstanding. The Form 10-Q has the detail, including the warrants issued with the financing.

Notes are shown at face value. The balance sheet carries the senior convertible notes at $3.2 million and the $11.0 million of convertible notes at $8.5 million, net of unamortized discounts, and the difference is recognized as non-cash interest expense over the life of the notes. The earnout is at the provisional fair value recorded in the Form 10-Q.

 

The $11.0 million of junior secured convertible notes were structured so that they can become preferred stock at the time of a listing, instead of being repaid in cash, and holders of the senior convertible notes may elect to convert to preferred stock on the same terms at the listing, so up to about $15 million of this can potentially become equity. The purchase price in the table is the part of the price for Advertise Purple that we agreed to pay over time. The cash we paid at closing, about $7.6 million, was funded largely with the proceeds of the senior and junior notes above. That is short-term funding: the junior notes convert to equity on a listing, and the senior notes can convert or be refinanced. The earnout is owed only if Advertise Purple reaches certain contractual milestones, and we intend to pay the seller note and the deferred payments from the cash the combined business generates.

 

None of that makes the debt go away. We ended the quarter with a working capital deficit of about $15.2 million, our filings continue to say there is substantial doubt about our ability to continue as a going concern, and a listing is not assured. If our listing does not happen, the $11.0 million of convertible notes will remain outstanding and mature in March 2028.

The companies are already working together

 

JUICE now runs Advertise Purple’s marketing and is bringing it new business, and Advertise Purple’s sales team sends leads to JUICE every month. We are bringing our technology, ONAR AI and Cortex, to Advertise Purple’s teams and clients. Advertise Purple continues to be led by its core team, five of whom signed employment agreements with us at the acquisition.

 

What comes next

 

We are pursuing a listing of our common stock on the Nasdaq Capital Market and are in the application and preparation stage. Approval is not assured. A listing requires a higher share price than ours, so a 1-for-250 reverse stock split was approved in October by our board of directors and by the required stockholder vote. The split is not yet effective. It changes the number of shares you hold, not your percentage of the company, and the $0.038576 conversion price of the Series 1 preferred stock adjusts by the same ratio.

 

The milestones to watch from here are the reverse split taking effect, Nasdaq’s decision on our application, and a senior credit facility to refinance the senior notes, none of which is assured, and then our annual report, the first that will include a full quarter of Advertise Purple.

 

A year ago, our third quarter revenue was $0.66 million. Counting Advertise Purple for the full quarter, on a pro forma basis this year’s third quarter revenue would have been $4.4 million. The first full quarter together with Advertise Purple is under way.

About ONAR Holding Corporation

ONAR (OTCID: ONAR), pronounced “honor,” is an AI-powered marketing platform. ONAR acquires specialist marketing companies serving middle-market and growth-stage brands across performance marketing, creative, and commerce, and operates them as one company on shared proprietary technology designed to automate up to 70% of manual marketing work. Its technology division, ONAR Labs, develops and houses the Company’s proprietary technology, including ONAR AI, a marketing intelligence platform deployed across the Company’s operating companies to improve productivity; Retina AI, a predictive customer intelligence platform; and Cortex, an offline and online sales attribution platform. ONAR continues to expand the platform through disciplined acquisitions, including JUICE, Scale Partner, and Advertise Purple. Learn more at www.onar.com.
