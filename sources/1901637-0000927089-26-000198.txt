USCB Financial Holdings, Inc. Announces Securities Portfolio Repositioning
Press Release
MIAMI, FL – November 25, 2026 – USCB Financial Holdings, Inc. (the “Company”) (NASDAQ: USCB), the holding company for U.S. Century Bank (the “Bank”), announced today that it sold approximately $22.4 million of available-for-sale (“AFS”) securities as part of a targeted balance sheet repositioning designed to improve recurring earnings, drive earnings per share (“EPS”) growth, and further strengthen profitability.
“Our strong capital position continues to give us the flexibility to proactively reshape the balance sheet and put our capital to work where it earns the most. This transaction is a deliberate step toward stronger recurring earnings and an improved net interest margin, which we believe will deliver greater value for the Company and our shareholders,” said Luis de la Aguilera, Chairman, President and CEO of the Company.
The AFS securities sold, consisting primarily of agency collateralized mortgage obligations and fixed-rate SBA securities, represented approximately 5.7% of the AFS portfolio, based on amortized cost as of September 30, 2026, and had a weighted average book yield of 1.56% and a weighted average life of approximately 6.6 years. The sale will result in an estimated one-time after-tax loss of approximately $3.7 million ($4.9 million pre-tax) that will be recorded in the fourth quarter of 2026. Assuming a 6.50% average yield on reinvestment of the approximately $17.5 million in sale proceeds, the repositioning is expected to increase net interest income by approximately $0.8 million in the first year, contribute approximately 3 basis points to annualized net interest margin beginning with the first quarter of 2027, and result in approximately $0.03 of estimated EPS accretion over the next four quarters.
In addition to improving yield, the repositioning shortens the expected average life of the redeployed assets to approximately 3.9 years, enhancing cash flow and reinvestment flexibility. Management believes that the execution of this strategy will position the Company well for continued profitable growth and will further enhance the value of the enterprise.
About USCB Financial Holdings, Inc.
USCB Financial Holdings, Inc. is the bank holding company for U.S. Century Bank. Established in 2002, U.S. Century Bank is one of the largest community banks headquartered in Miami, and one of the largest community banks in the State of Florida. U.S. Century Bank is rated 5-Stars by BauerFinancial, the nation’s leading independent bank rating firm. U.S. Century Bank offers customers a wide range of financial products and services and supports numerous community organizations, including the Greater Miami Chamber of Commerce, the South Florida Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, and ChamberSouth.
