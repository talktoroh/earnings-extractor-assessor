Stewards Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Provides Business Update
• Total assets increased 49% from year-end to $265.3 million; stockholders’ equity totaled $40.8 million
• Completed the $79 million refinancing of 1818 Park and acquired Envy Pompano Beach, expanding the company’s South Florida real assets portfolio
• Digital customer portal launched, and the Plaid integration is operating within the underwriting workflow
• Stewards Business Card development underway with Rain; initial availability to qualified customers targeted for early in the first quarter of 2027
FORT LAUDERDALE, Fla., Nov. 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Stewards, Inc. (Nasdaq: SWRD) ("Stewards" or the "company") today reported financial results for the third quarter ended Sept. 30, 2026, and provided an update on its operating and capital priorities. The company expects to file its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q after the market closes today.
“The third quarter marked a major step forward for Stewards. We began trading on Nasdaq, completed the $79 million refinancing of 1818 Park, acquired Envy and grew total assets to $265.3 million. We also launched our digital customer portal, put the Plaid integration into operation and advanced the Stewards Business Card with Rain. We have moved quickly to expand the platform across real assets, private credit and technology. Our focus now is turning that expansion into results by improving property performance, increasing private credit deployment as funding capacity expands and moving the Business Card toward initial availability for qualified customers,” said Shaun Quin, chief executive officer.
Third Quarter and Recent Business Highlights
Unless otherwise stated, comparisons are against the prior-year period. Financial information is unaudited and presented in U.S. dollars.
• Began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the ticker SWRD on Sept. 10, 2026.
• Completed the $79.0 million refinancing of 1818 Park on July 24, 2026, and acquired Envy Pompano Beach on Sept. 23. Envy contributed results from its closing through quarter-end.
• Total assets increased 49% to $265.3 million as of Sept. 30, 2026, from $178.4 million at Dec. 31, 2025. Total liabilities were $216.0 million, mezzanine equity was $8.5 million and stockholders' equity was $40.8 million.
• Income from rental property increased 5% to $2.4 million. Total third-quarter revenue was $3.4 million, compared with $5.3 million a year earlier, reflecting lower private credit deployment, partially offset by higher income from rental property.
• Reported a third-quarter net loss of $19.4 million, compared with $6.4 million a year earlier. The quarter included an $8.5 million noncash change in the fair value of warrant liabilities, $1.6 million of noncash depreciation and amortization and $4.4 million of interest expense.
Business Update
Private Credit
Since 2020, Stewards has originated more than $153 million in funding and supported more than 10,000 businesses. Private credit deployment was lower during the quarter as the company managed available capital and directed capital toward expanding its real estate portfolio.
Real Assets
Income from rental property increased to $2.4 million in the third quarter from $2.3 million a year earlier. Envy closed on Sept. 23, and its third-quarter results reflect the period from closing through quarter-end. As of Sept. 30, 1818 Park was 93.0% occupied and 94.9% leased, while Envy was 90.2% occupied and 93.0% leased.
Larry Botel, president of Stewards Realty, leads the recently integrated JOSS Realty Partners team in overseeing asset management across the portfolio. Botel and the JOSS team bring experience spanning 32 acquisitions totaling approximately 3.4 million square feet and more than $1.2 billion in value. Crown Residential has been engaged to manage both 1818 Park and Envy. The operating focus is improving occupancy and collections, managing concessions, reducing costs, leasing available retail space and evaluating applicable property tax incentives.
This work supports Stewards’ broader real estate strategy of generating recurring rental income and establishing an equity base that may support future financing while retaining the underlying properties. Any future financing would depend on eligible equity, property values, lender consent, terms and required approvals.
Technology and the Stewards Business Card
Stewards launched its digital customer portal during the quarter, giving business owners one place to submit information, upload documents and review funding opportunities. The Plaid integration is operating within the underwriting workflow and supports bank data review. Together, the portal and Plaid integration support a single digital application and underwriting process.
Following quarter-end, Stewards announced a partnership with Rain to develop the Stewards Business Card, which is intended to provide qualified customers with recurring access to approved capital for business purchases. Stewards expects to manage the customer relationship, underwriting and financing decisions, while Rain is expected to provide card issuance, payment infrastructure and settlement. Initial availability to qualified customers is targeted for early in the first quarter of 2027, subject to integration, testing, compliance and required approvals. After launch, the company intends to report account activations and transaction activity.

Capital Structure and Liquidity
At Sept. 30, 2026, Stewards had $2.0 million in cash and cash equivalents. An additional $7.4 million was held in restricted, lender-required reserve accounts. Current liabilities exceeded current assets by $25.8 million, including the $20.1 million carrying amount of the Envy required-redemption liability that Stewards agreed to restructure after quarter-end, as described below. Because the September Nasdaq listing was a direct uplisting, the company did not receive primary capital proceeds in connection with the listing.

Capital and Liability Actions After Quarter-End
• Noteholder standstill and interest deferral: On Oct. 30, 2026, holders of approximately $31.8 million of Stewards’ 15% secured promissory notes confirmed their approval of a waiver of defaults arising from the timing of interest payments, a standstill with respect to unpaid interest through Dec. 31, 2027, and the deferral of cash interest payments. The company is drafting formal agreements to document these terms.
• Envy amendment: After quarter-end, Stewards entered into an amendment to the Envy acquisition terms under which, upon effectiveness, the related $21.0 million contractual cash-redemption obligation will be satisfied by exchanging 7.0 million escrowed common shares for 7.0 million shares of Series D convertible preferred stock. Stewards will also issue a $1.4 million unsecured note for late-payment damages and the late charge on the $2.0 million Envy note. The amendment will become effective upon satisfaction of the remaining conditions, including receipt of a required lender consent, the filing and acceptance of the Series D certificate of designation and release of the escrowed common shares for cancellation. Because the amendment was entered into after quarter-end, it is not reflected in the Sept. 30, 2026, balance sheet. The Series D shares are convertible one-for-one into common stock.
• October financing: On Oct. 28, 2026, Stewards issued a $6.05 million convertible note for a $5.5 million purchase price and received approximately $4.9 million in net proceeds. Because the financing closed after quarter-end, the proceeds are not included in the Sept. 30 cash balance. The note includes conversion, commitment-share and mandatory-prepayment provisions. Any additional notes require mutual agreement.
The warrants, Series D preferred shares, convertible note and related commitment shares may result in dilution to existing shareholders.
The Form 10-Q expected to be filed after the market closes today will include additional information about the company's liquidity, financing obligations and the actions described above.
Near-Term Execution Priorities
• Private Credit: Advance the current product pipeline, expand partner and direct-to-merchant origination, and increase deployment in line with funding capacity while maintaining underwriting discipline.
• Real Assets: Improve occupancy, collections and operating efficiency at 1818 Park and Envy while pursuing retail leasing and evaluating applicable property tax incentives.
• Stewards Business Card: Advance integration, testing and compliance work toward targeted initial availability to qualified customers early in the first quarter of 2027, subject to required approvals.
• Capital Structure: Complete formal documentation of the noteholder waiver, standstill and cash interest deferral, and advance other capital structure initiatives.

About Stewards
Stewards, Inc. (Nasdaq: SWRD) is a diversified financial platform operating across private credit, real assets and technology-enabled operations. Through Stewards Business Capital, the company provides revenue-based financing to small and midsize businesses through an established origination, underwriting and servicing platform. Stewards' Real Assets business owns and operates a portfolio of multifamily, mixed-use and commercial real estate assets, while the company continues to develop technology designed to improve operating efficiency and connectivity across its businesses. Stewards is headquartered in Fort Lauderdale, Florida. For more information, visit stewards.com.
