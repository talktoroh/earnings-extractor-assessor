Prairie Operating Co. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results
Houston, Texas, November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Prairie Operating Co. (Nasdaq: PROP) (the “Company,” “Prairie,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) – an independent energy company engaged in the development and acquisition of oil, natural gas, and natural gas liquids (“NGL”) resources in the Denver-Julesburg (DJ) Basin – today announced its financial and operational results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Results Summary
•
Sales volumes of 2.4 MMBoe, or 26,117 Boe/d, a total volume increase of 21% from the prior quarter, with 77% liquids (56% oil).
•
Revenue of $129.5 million, an increase of 31% from the prior quarter.
•
Net loss attributable to Prairie Operating Co. common stockholders of $34.0 million, or $0.26 per basic and $0.28 per diluted share.
•
Generated Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $65.3 million, an increase of 92% from the prior quarter.
•
Lease operating expense per Boe of $5.71, a decrease of 17% from the prior quarter.
•
Exit rate production of approximately 30,229 Boe/d.
•
General and administrative expenses decreased 24% from the prior quarter to $4.56 per Boe.
Key Highlights for Year-to-Date 2026
•
Sales volumes of 6.5 MMBoe, or approximately 23,730 Boe/d, with 74% liquids (52% oil).
•
All 27 wells drilled across four pads were online at September 30, 2026.
•
Total revenue of $311.8 million, an increase of approximately 97% year-over-year.
•
Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $136.4 million, an increase of approximately 37% year-over-year.
•
Brought Burnett online at the end of August and Castor online at the end of September.
•
During the third quarter, extended the issuance date of the Series F Preferred Stock anniversary warrants to December 1, 2026.
•
Subsequent to quarter-end, production reached approximately 32,287 Boe/d.
(1) Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” for the definition and a reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP financial measures used in this press release.
Greg Patton, Chief Executive Officer, commented:
“Prairie’s third-quarter results demonstrate the strength of our assets and the excellent work our team continues to deliver across our development program. Average daily sales volumes increased 21% from the prior quarter to 2.4 MMboe or 26,117 Boe per day, driving revenue growth of 31% from the prior quarter to $129.5 million. We achieved that growth while reducing lease operating expense per Boe by 17% from the prior quarter, reflecting our continued focus on improving well performance and operating efficiency.”
“Our Burnett development is a significant step forward in demonstrating the value of Prairie’s acreage. Its production results supported the conversion of 30 gross possible locations to proved undeveloped locations within Genesis OGDP I, expanding our proved inventory and reducing uncertainty around nearby development. By bringing 37 wells across five pads online by the quarter-end, we have a stronger production base and even greater confidence in the opportunities ahead.”
Michael Shelly, Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, added:
“Prairie generated $65.3 million of Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter, an increase of 92% from the prior quarter, as sales volumes increased and unit operating costs declined. With Burnett and Castor now producing, our focus is on realizing the cash returns from the capital invested this year and carefully managing the spending required to sustain that production.”
“We continue to evaluate opportunities to refinance the remaining Series F preferred stock as part of our broader effort to strengthen Prairie’s balance sheet, improve financial flexibility and better align our capital structure with the scale of our operations. Our objective is to support Prairie’s operating momentum through disciplined financial management and capital allocation that creates sustainable, long-term shareholder value.”

Operations Update
During the third quarter of 2026, Prairie recorded sales volumes of 2,403 MBoe, an increase of 21% from the prior quarter, which equates to average daily sales volumes of 26,117 Boe/d. Oil represented approximately 56% of sales volumes, and liquids represented approximately 77%.
Operated and non-operated production reached a third-quarter peak of approximately 30,229 Boe/d at the end of September. As a result of the wells being turned in line during the third quarter, production continued to increase following quarter-end, with production reaching approximately 32,287 Boe/d by early October.
Year to date, Prairie has drilled 27 wells across four pads, with 19 wells drilled in a single run. Year-to-date wells averaged approximately 18,700 feet in measured depth, an average rate of penetration of 380 feet per hour and an average spud-to-rig-release time of 6.2 days.
The four-well Burnett pad came online at the end of August, delivering successful initial production results that validate the quality and future development potential of Prairie’s acreage. Maximum initial production averaged approximately 722 Boe/d per well, with 81% oil on a two-stream basis, and cumulative gross production reached 131,490 Boe through October 20, 2026.
Completion activities on the six-well Castor pad were finalized in September, with the wells coming online at the end of September. The late-quarter start limited Castor’s contribution to third-quarter average production.
The strong drilling, completion and early production performance supported the conversion of 30 gross possible locations to proved undeveloped locations within the 5,700-acre Genesis OGDP I. These results reinforce the commercial viability of nearby undeveloped locations, de-risk Prairie’s near-term development inventory and provide greater confidence in future development across the project area. The Company remains focused on well performance, operating efficiency and disciplined capital allocation.

About Prairie Operating Co.
Prairie Operating Co. is a Houston-based publicly traded independent energy company engaged in the development and acquisition of oil, natural gas, and natural gas liquid resources in the United States. The Company’s assets and operations are concentrated in the oil and liquids-rich regions of the Denver-Julesburg (DJ) Basin, with a primary focus on the Niobrara and Codell formations. The Company is committed to the responsible development of its oil natural gas, and natural gas liquid resources and is focused on maximizing returns through consistent growth, capital discipline, and sustainable cash flow generation.
More information about the Company can be found at www.prairieopco.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Reconciliation of Net (Loss) Income to Adjusted EBITDA
Adjusted EBITDA is a supplemental non-GAAP financial measure that is used by management to evaluate the performance of our business, make operational decisions, and assess our ability to generate cashflows. Management believes Adjusted EBITDA provides investors with helpful information to better understand the underlying performance trends of our business, facilitate period-to-period comparisons, and assess the company’s operating results.
Adjusted EBITDA is derived from net (loss) income attributable to Prairie Operating Co. and represents earnings before depreciation, depletion, and amortization, abandonment and impairment of unproved properties, non-cash stock-based compensation, interest expense, net, unrealized loss (gain) on derivatives, non-cash (gain) loss on adjustment to fair value – financial instrument liabilities, litigation and severance settlement expense, and income tax benefit, all as applicable. We present Adjusted EBITDA because the adjustments listed above can vary substantially between periods and companies within our industry depending upon accounting methods, book values of assets, capital structures, and the method by which assets were acquired. Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool, because it excludes certain items that affect our reported financial results. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income calculated in accordance with GAAP or as an indicator of our operating performance or liquidity. Additionally, our calculation of Adjusted EBITDA may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.
