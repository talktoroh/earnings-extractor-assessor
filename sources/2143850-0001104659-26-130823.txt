City Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Highlights
Phase 1 data for CITY-FXI selected for oral presentation at ASH 2026; nonclinical data supporting advancement of CITY-TFR2 into clinical development to be presented in poster session
First participant dosed in Phase 1 trial of CITY-RBP4 for treatment of Stargardt disease
Clinical data expected from all three lead programs in 2027
Completed upsized $230 million IPO, extending expected cash runway into 2029
CAMBRIDGE, Mass., November 23, 2026 – City Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: CTY), a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing the next generation of RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided business updates.
“City is entering a milestone-rich period, with data readouts anticipated from each of our three lead programs over the coming year,” said Andy Orth, President and Chief Executive Officer of City Therapeutics. “That momentum begins at ASH in December, with an oral presentation highlighting data from our ongoing Phase 1 trial of CITY-FXI for thromboembolic diseases, alongside nonclinical data supporting the advancement of CITY-TFR2 for anemia of chronic disease into clinical development. With the Phase 1 trial of CITY-RBP4 for Stargardt disease now underway, we are making excellent progress across our clinical pipeline. We believe our successful IPO positions us to build on this progress and harness the potential of our City Platform to expand the reach of RNAi therapeutics.”
Recent Business and Pipeline Highlights
· | Completed upsized initial public offering. In October 2026, City completed its upsized initial public offering, including the full exercise of the underwriters’ option to purchase additional shares, raising gross proceeds of approximately $230.0 million, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses. City’s common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Market on October 16, 2026, under the ticker symbol “CTY.”
· | Initiated Phase 1 clinical trial of CITY-RBP4. Earlier this month, City announced dosing of the first participant in its Phase 1 clinical trial evaluating single and multiple subcutaneous doses of CITY-RBP4 in healthy volunteers and people with Stargardt disease type 1 (Stargardt disease).
Anticipated Milestones
City remains on track to achieve its previously disclosed development milestones, with clinical data expected in 2027 from all three lead programs.
CITY-FXI: Phase 1 program for the prevention of thromboembolic diseases
· | Present data from the ongoing Phase 1 trial of CITY-FXI, including additional data from single ascending dose (SAD) cohorts of healthy volunteers and initial data from the first SAD cohort of adults with inherited risk factors for thrombosis, in an oral presentation at the 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting on December 12, 2026.
· | Report additional data from Phase 1 SAD cohorts in healthy volunteers and adults with inherited prothrombotic risk factors in mid-2027.
· | Initiate Phase 2 proof-of-concept trial in patients undergoing total knee arthroplasty in mid-2027, with data expected in early 2028.
· | Report data from the multiple ascending dose (MAD) portion of Phase 1 trial in adults with history of provoked venous thromboembolism with enduring risk factors in late 2027.
CITY-RBP4: Phase 1 program for treatment of Stargardt disease
· | Report initial data from Phase 1 trial in mid-2027.
· | Report SAD data in healthy volunteers and people with Stargardt disease and multiple dose data in people with Stargardt disease from the Phase 1 trial in early 2028.
· | Initiate a Phase 2 clinical trial in geographic atrophy in late 2027.
CITY-TFR2: RNAi therapeutic candidate for the treatment of anemia of chronic disease
· | Present nonclinical data supporting the clinical development of CITY-TFR2 at ASH.
· | Initiate investigator-initiated trial (IIT) in China in patients with anemia associated with myelofibrosis in late 2026, with data expected in mid-2027.
· | File investigational new drug application or equivalent in late 2026 and initiate a Phase 1/2 trial in early 2027, with data expected in late 2027.
· | Initiate a Phase 2 trial in an additional indication of anemia of chronic disease in early 2028, subject to results from the IIT and Phase 1/2 trial.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Cash position: Cash and cash equivalents were $142.0 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $112.8 million as of December 31, 2025. Following the completion of its upsized IPO in October 2026, City expects its cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, together with the net proceeds from the offering, to fund its currently planned operations into 2029.
Collaboration revenue: Collaboration revenue was $7.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $6.6 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $1.0 million was primarily due to higher revenue recognized under City’s research collaboration and license agreement with Biogen, partially offset by lower revenue recognized under its research collaboration and license agreement with Bausch + Lomb.
Research and development expenses: Research and development (R&D) expenses were $25.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $17.0 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $8.5 million was primarily due to increased costs to support the advancement of CITY-TFR2, investment in the City Platform and discovery stage assets, and increased personnel and facilities costs, partially offset by decreased costs in the quarter for CITY-FXI, CITY-RBP4 and City’s collaboration programs.
General and administrative expenses: General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $5.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $3.3 million for the same period in 2025. The increase of $2.5 million was primarily due to an increase in professional fees, personnel costs, and other general and administrative costs to support City’s organizational growth.
Net loss: Net loss was $23.9 million, or $3.03 per share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared with a net loss of $14.0 million, or $2.63 per share, for the same period in 2025. The increase of $9.9 million was primarily due to higher R&D and G&A expenses, partially offset by higher collaboration revenue.
All share and per share amounts for all periods presented have been retroactively adjusted to give effect to the 1.0983-for-1 stock split of the City Therapeutics common stock effected on October 8, 2026.
About City Therapeutics
City Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company engineering the next generation of RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics, with the aim of delivering meaningful and durable benefits for patients in need. City’s lead development programs are CITY-FXI for thromboembolic diseases, CITY-RBP4 for Stargardt disease and other retinal diseases, and CITY-TFR2 for anemia associated with chronic diseases. The City Platform is the engine behind every therapeutic the company develops and integrates deep expertise in trigger engineering, including proprietary cityRNA triggers designed to address limitations of conventional RNAi approaches, tissue-selective delivery, and genetics-driven target selection. For more information, visit www.citytx.com and follow us on LinkedIn, X, and Bluesky.
